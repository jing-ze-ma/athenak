## 0. What is missing today

`src/rad_m1/` reads only `pmy_pack->phydro` (u0, u1, eos, use_etotgrav, phicc0, the ghost
exchange + ConToPrim of `HydroConToPrim`). `pmhd` appears nowhere. The pgens

## 1. Which fluid arrays M1 addresses under MHD

- Conserved `pmhd->u0` has the hydro layout: IDN, IM1..IM3, IEN (+ scalars). IEN is the
  TOTAL energy e_int + KE + |B|^2/2 (+ rho Phi with `<mhd>/etotgrav`).
- `u1` (U^n copy; MHD::CopyCons also copies b0 -> b1 at stage 1), `w0` (w0(IEN) = e_int,
  as in hydro), `wtemp`, `peos->eos_data` (same EOS_Data type; general EOS + table in
  general_mhd.cpp), `use_etotgrav`, `phicc0`, `phi0`, `wbq0`, `use_wellbalance_dynamic`,
  `use_wb_x1`, `wb_option`, `uflx`, `pcond` (same Conduction class, reads its own block)
  exist on MHD with the same names.
- Missing on MHD: `use_phi_wb`, `phicc_wb`, `phi_wb_x1f`, `EnableWBEffectivePotential`,
  `SetWBEffectivePotential` (section 4).
- B: face field `b0` (U^n copy `b1`), cell field `bcc0`. The C2P builds the cell field
  from b0 (general_mhd.cpp 108-139, ideal_mhd.cpp 80-110): Cartesian: plain face mean in
  all three directions; spherical polar: the x1v/x2v/x3v-weighted mean in ALL THREE
  directions (lw = (xf_{i+1} - xv_i)/dx, rw = (xv_i - xf_i)/dx); cubed sphere with a
  stretched x1: CellCenteredRadialFld in x1. M1 must subtract EXACTLY that |bcc|^2/2 so
  its temperature equals the C2P's.

## 2. Mapping of the coupling terms

- Every M1 kernel that forms the gas internal energy does `eg = u(IEN) - KE - egrv`,
  egrv = rho Phi (etotgrav) or 0. Under MHD egrv becomes the non-thermal, non-kinetic
  energy in IEN: rho Phi + emag, emag = |bcc|^2/2. The write-back
  `u(IEN) = eg + KE + egrv + work` then restores emag unchanged. The solve itself works
  on EGN (the internal energy) and never sees B.
- Energy exchange: changes e_int only. Radiation force: IM1..3 only. Work: KE change.
  Implicit velocity (vimp) and advect_split velocity: m/rho from u0. None of these change.
- B does not enter the grey M1 equations (no Lorentz / plasma coupling): only the energy
  bookkeeping of IEN sees it. M1 never writes b0, so within one M1 call emag is constant
  per cell; it changes only in MHD stages (CT) and in the host restores b0 <- b1.
- Hydro: the added term is guarded by `if (mhd)` (a runtime bool captured by value), so
  the hydro arithmetic is unchanged operation for operation (bitwise).

## 3. Accessor and emag (one code path)

- `struct FluidRef` (new header `src/rad_m1/m1_fluid.hpp`, also included by the two
  pgens): a host-side POD built by `FluidRef::Get(pmbp)` from phydro or pmhd: views u0,
  u1, w0, wtemp, uflx, phicc0, phi0, wbq0, phicc_wb, phi_wb_x1f; flags use_etotgrav,
  use_wellbalance_dynamic, use_wb_x1, use_phi_wb, wb_option; EOS_Data; Conduction
  *pcond; `bool mhd`; the two raw pointers for host dispatch (ConToPrim,
  EnableWBEffectivePotential, the u-only ghost refresh). Kernels copy the members they
  need into locals exactly as today (`auto uh = fl.u0;`).
- emag storage: two 4-D arrays on the M1 module, allocated only under MHD (1x1x1x1 dummy
  for hydro, never read): `emag0` = |bcc(b0)|^2/2 of the current state, `emag1` = of the
  U^n state (b1). Filled by one kernel `EmagBuild(b, emag)` over all cells incl. ghosts
  with the C2P's averaging (sect. 1) -- NOT from pmhd->bcc0, which is stale after host
  restores and does not exist for b1.
  - `emag0` refreshed at the top of `Opacity` (before its early returns; it is the first
    gas-reading task of both M1 chains and precedes Coupling / ImplicitSolve in the same
    list), in `Time2Restore` and the MR restore (after b0 <- b1), in `MRSolve` after the
    Y_0 copy, and in `Time2VetColAt` (defensive, one kernel).
  - `emag1` refreshed in `Time2VetStart` (U^n = u1/b1) before its kernels.
  - The swap trick `std::swap(ph->u0, ph->u1); Opacity(); swap back` (rad_m1_time2.cpp
    505-507) becomes `SwapU01 + std::swap(emag0, emag1)` around an Opacity call that
    must not rebuild emag0 (member flag `emag_hold`).
- Cost (MHD only): one light kernel (6 face reads, 1 write per cell) per refresh and one
  extra 4-D read in each gas kernel; an implicit solve is tens of sweeps: < 1 % expected.
  Hydro: no kernel, one never-taken branch per gas kernel (GPU timing gate, sect. 7).

## 4. WB effective potential (force_reference = wb_arad) and `split` under MHD

- Port to MHD exactly as hydro (hydro.hpp 181-187, 297-306; hydro.cpp 41-42, 218-221;
  hydro_wellbalance.cpp 294-295, 335-395): members `use_phi_wb`, `phicc_wb`,
  `phi_wb_x1f` (shallow aliases of phicc0 / phi0.x1f unless enabled, so the default MHD
  path, incl. dhj and cs production, is bitwise), EnableWBEffectivePotential,
  SetWBEffectivePotential; readers switched: mhd_wellbalance.cpp 225-226 (BuildWBCache)
  and the x1 WB reconstruction calls in mhd_fluxes.cpp 85/87 (locals), 186-187 (w0),
  191-192 (b0: WB not applied to b, `hyd = false`), 218, 262, 284. x2/x3 (508, 553) keep
  the true potential, as in hydro.
- Magnetic pressure and the WB background: the K&M background balances the GAS pressure
  against g (or g_eff). A uniform field (box) exerts no force. The wedge's split monopole
  B_r ~ r^-2 is current-free (J x B = 0 analytically), but its discrete Maxwell-stress
  divergence + sp geometric source balances only to truncation, a residual force
  O(dx^2) B^2/r. Gate (iii) measures it (weak and moderate B); no WB for B is added.
- `split` (ke-dt-0926): the pgen WB source adds rho arad_ref v1 (stage velocity) to IEN;
  the implicit fws kernel (rad_m1_implicit.cpp 8752-8784) touches IM1/IEN increments only.
  Nothing B-specific; only the pgen sources must run on the MHD u0/w0.

## 5. Ordering, communication and restart (items the draft missed)

- Stage ordering (driver.cpp 461-585): M1 runs AFTER the whole fluid stage (hesdirk2:
  after each Heun stage's after_stagen; split/MR/BE paths: after the RK loop and after the
  MHD RKG resistivity sub-loop). The MHD CT update of b0, the B ghost exchange and the MHD
  ConToPrim (bcc0) are complete, so emag0 from b0 is the stage's field. The next MHD
  stage's EMFs use w0 from the M1 refresh (velocity changed by the radiation force):
  consistent, as in hydro. No CT/EMF reordering needed.
- Ghost refresh after M1 (rad_m1_coupling.cpp 380-395 `HydroConToPrim`, rad_m1_time2.cpp
  390-401 `Time2Restore`, rad_m1_mr.cpp 307-323 MR restore): the hydro form calls
  InitRecv(-1)/SendU/ClearSend(-1)/ClearRecv(-1)/RecvU. MHD::InitRecv(-1) ALSO posts the
  B receives (mhd_tasks.cpp 128-133) and MHD::ClearRecv(-1) MPI_Waits on them (875-881,
  bvals_tasks.cpp 97-122): B is never sent there -> DEADLOCK on > 1 rank. The MHD form
  drives `pmhd->pbval_u` directly (InitRecv(nmhd+nscalars), PackAndSendCC(u0,
  coarse_u0), ClearSend, ClearRecv, RecvAndUnpackCC), then MHD::RestrictU (u only),
  ApplyPhysicalBCs (BFieldBCs on an unchanged b0 + pgen user BC: harmless), Prolongate,
  ConToPrim. One helper `FluidRefresh(pdrive)` replaces the three copies (the hydro call
  sequence is kept verbatim -> bitwise).
- Restores b0 <- b1 (Time2Restore, MR stage failure): b1 is the stage-1 copy of b0 incl.
  ghosts (valid), so no B exchange is needed. MRSolve's Y_0 copy u1 <- u0 also does
  b1 <- b0 (MHD u1/b1 are free between steps: CopyCons refills them at stage 1).
- ConToPrim / FOFC: MHD FOFC acts inside the MHD flux/update of the stage; M1 comes after
  and its refresh runs the ordinary MHD C2P (floors and the eint_rst cache handled inside
  MHD::ConToPrim 730-760 as in hydro). No interplay beyond hydro's.
- Restart: restart.cpp writes MHD u0 + b0 (+ MHD wtemp/wder/eint for a general EOS)
  before the M1 block; pgen.cpp 833-969 reads them before M1 (1013+); the M1 slope block
  (Time2RstPack: E, F, gas slopes of IM/IEN) carries no B. Layout already consistent. The
  pgens must redo EnableWBEffectivePotential + phi_eff on an MHD restart (they do for
  hydro) and must not re-apply the B IC.
- sp face geometry: the monopole IC is set on x1 faces as b0.x1f = B0 (r0/x1f)^2 (flux
  r^2 B_r constant -> div B = 0 exactly with the face areas); x2f = x3f = 0. Uniform box
  field: b0.x?f = const. User BCs fill b0 ghost faces (box: the uniform value /
  zero-gradient copy; wedge: B0 (r0/x1f)^2 on the x1 ghost faces), and the ghost IEN adds
  emag of the ghost bcc (faces first, then cells).
- Guards: `<mhd>` + `<hydro>` (ion-neutral) with `<rad_m1>` -> FATAL (ambiguous fluid);
  cubed sphere + MHD + M1 -> FATAL until tested; eos checks read the fluid's own block
  ("hydro" or "mhd"): isothermal, eos_radiation.

radiative column stays static (box uniform B, wedge monopole; beta 1e4 and 10).

weak field. (v) div B at machine level, restart bitwise, 1 vs 2 GPUs round-off (the
