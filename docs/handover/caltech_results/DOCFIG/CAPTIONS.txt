Captions for the documentation figures and movies (NOTE 2026-11-26o items 3-4, 26p). One node = 4 H200 GPUs. Plain numbers only; no claim on convergence or resolution.
Panels of every star figure: left v_r [km/s] on one shell (cmasher fusion), middle T_eff [10^3 K] of each column from the comoving flux at its tau_R = 2/3 (bsg: tau_R = 1) (cmocean ice, low dark, high light), right density relative to the shell mean, delta rho/<rho>, on a radial-lateral slice (cmocean balance, x and y in R_sun; grey: floor gas, density at most 1.5 x the floor). Axis scales are in R_sun and degrees.
