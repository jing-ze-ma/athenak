Panels of every star figure: left v_r [km/s] at tau_R = 2/3 in each column (cmasher fusion; linear between cell centres, no smoothing; columns without a photosphere would be grey: none in any dump shown), middle T_eff [10^3 K] of each column from the comoving flux at its tau_R = 2/3 (tau_R = 2/3 for all five stars, bsg too) (cmocean ice, low dark, high light), right density relative to the shell mean, delta rho/<rho>, on a radial-lateral slice (cmocean balance, x and y in R_sun; grey: floor gas, density at most 1.5 x the floor). Axis scales are in R_sun and degrees.
