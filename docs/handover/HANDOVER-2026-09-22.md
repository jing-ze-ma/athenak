## 2. THE OPEN PROBLEM (start here)

The SEEDED 2-D He slab (84 x 32, production entropy seed) is not physical: KE_1 grows at
0.16-0.19 /s, ~85x the convective rate v_MLT/H_p = 2.1e-3 /s, in EVERY coupling arm
(radiative force off, heating off, transverse force off); by 200 s the flow is supersonic
and F1top/Fin = 0.39.  The static slab is quiet, the solver converges, T10 passes.
A diagnostic agent was running at the time of writing (`tests_m1/runs_3b7/`, arms:
hydro-only with the frozen reference acceleration, 1-D seed, amplitude and CFL scans,
unfixed split, top cut at tau ~ 1, consistent seed; depth profiles of the growth).
My leading hypothesis (H1): carrying the radiative acceleration as a fixed body force
`a_rad_ref(z)` makes the GAS-ONLY stratification strongly Schwarzschild-unstable
(`dlnT/dlnP_gas = nabla_tot * beta * g/g_eff` exceeds 0.4 where beta g/g_eff > ~1.3), and
the residual force + thermal coupling do not restore the mixture's weak buoyancy at that
growth rate.  If confirmed, the well-balanced reference is not a neutral bookkeeping
device in a radiation-dominated convection zone and the formulation must change (time-
updated horizontal-mean reference, or a WB scheme aware of the instantaneous radiative
force).  Other suspects: the WB effective potential in multi-D, the optically thin top
(positivity fallback fires on almost every step), the seed being out of radiative
equilibrium by construction.  Read `tests_m1/runs_3b7/RESULTS.txt` first.
