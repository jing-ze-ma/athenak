### Why the L_in history column reads ~10x too high (diagnosis only; no code changed)
- L_in for he_bc_linhao is `lbath_f x area` (he_star_m1.cpp ~4297; rad_m1_implicit.cpp 18522-18543): `lbath_f = fn + adf`.
  - `fn` = the half-range face flux, about 0.5 (F_g + F_is) = the target.
  - `adf = 0.5 [a_g1 E_g1 + a_is E_is]` with a = (4/3) v_r: the radiation-enthalpy advection through the wall, using the zero-contact first-ghost v_r and the first-cell v_r.
- At the base E = aT^4 is about 5e10 erg/cc, so c E/F is about 2e7. A wall velocity of only ~0.1 km/s makes (4/3) v E equal to 10 F.
- The start-up acoustic sloshing at the wall (history v1sq_wall) has v_rms of 1.5e4 cm/s at 11 ks, falling to 7e3 cm/s at 77 ks (dayF512r). (4/3) v_rms E_wall A_wall = 1.2e40 and 5.5e39. The measured L_in - L_target is 0.93e40 and 4.0e39, so they agree to 20-30 %.
- **It is NOT only a diagnostic.** The total-energy history follows the integral of (L_in - L_top), not the integral of (L_target - L_top):

| run | dEtot | int (L_in - L_top) | int (L_target - L_top) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dayF (1 d) | +1.27e45 | +1.55e45 | -1.2e44 |
| dayF512r (1 d) | +1.54e44 | +4.3e44 | -1.3e44 |
| longA_LIC (2.8 d) | +2.61e45 | +2.89e45 | -1.2e44 |

- So the advective radiation-enthalpy flux at the zero-mass-flux wall injects real energy: about 1.8e39 erg/s net at 512 radial, about 2 L_wedge on day 1, decaying with v_rms. The gas mass flux there is about 0 (Mdot_bot ~1e10 g/s).
- Likely origin: the first ghost's v_r is chosen so that the HLLC gas contact speed is zero, not so that v_g E_g + v_is E_is = 0. The radiation therefore sees a moving face where the gas does not.
- The same construction is in the BSG reproduction deck (Ma's Athena++ uses ComToLab with v_ghost). Worth a look before production (e.g. drop adf at the wall, or use the face contact speed 0 for the radiation advection). Not changed: needs the user's OK.
