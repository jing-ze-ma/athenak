## 4. ADDENDA

### 4.1 The implicit transverse radiative-diffusion operator
`rad_cap_ang` (the explicit transverse cap) was found to be **a spurious energy sink**, not just a
throttle: narrow 336x8x8 one-rank arms (`bench/bstar_fecz/deepcool/`) give F_top/F_bot = 1.21-1.24 within
200 s and E -0.58 % in 600 s with cap 0.5 + vpert, box-wide `<v1>` -3e4 cm/s; with vpert 0, or with
cap 0 + the operator, or in 1-D, it is 1.000.  Mechanism: unrelaxed horizontal T structure x nonlinear
kappa(T,rho) -- F ~ T^3/kappa dT/dz raises the horizontally averaged flux and energy leaves through the
top.  **Every capped box result is compromised** (the cap inflated the He horizontal KE 3-4x).
**Rule: FeCZ boxes run `rad_cap_ang = 0` + `rad_implicit_ang = true`.**

The operator (`rad_implicit_ang`, RKL1 super-time-stepping on the energy increment, coefficients reused
from the cap arrays) replaces it.  **A bug found and fixed during development** (be12cf30 / 7ffa2566):
the operator split linearised about `T*` taken from `w0` at the *stage start* rather than the
post-radial energy it writes on (4-7 % per stage), which ran away 1.5x/cycle at the bottom row; the fix
refreshes `T*`/alpha from `u0` (Gaussian order 2.27 -> 1.65, absolute error 2-3x better; refreshing the
face conductance `C_f` too was left for a later step).  GPU + MPI bitwise; cost 1.25x per cycle in the
B-star box, which is why PCG was deferred.  Operator confirmed exactly 2nd order (test regate 505f995b).

**Unified RKL1 (`rad_sts_all`, main tree 75a3ce3c..271bcf6a)**: one RKL1 loop over x1+x2+x3 replaces
"implicit radial + RKL1 transverse".  **4.4x more accurate than the split at the same dt**, 25 % more
substages, conservative to 3e-16; physical x1 faces stay explicit.  Cartesian isotropic boxes only --
**not** stretched spherical grids.  Test `test_rad_sts_all_cpu.py`.

Speed switches, all bitwise off, all measured: `rad_sts_split` (explicit part of each face conductance
inside the RK stages) gives L1 13-26 % better on uniformly stiff tests but is **2.2x SLOWER in the B-star
box** at the same substage count and an identical solution (5e-6) -> **leave off**; `rad_sts_once`
(one budget per cycle) is 40 % fewer substages and 11 % wall but **L1 4x worse** -> leave off;
`rad_sts_margin` (integer quantised) is ~0 -> leave off.  Details in
`implicit-transverse-raddiff-plan.md`.

  balance; `rt2turn` lost **3.1 % of E in 3500 s** (15-20 % of F*A) out of the top, drained from the deep

  F_top diagnostic is biased a few % when dtau/cell ~ 1 -- **the hst energy budget `1-(dE/dt)/(A F)` is

### 4.4 The He box: the organ-pipe mode is dt-linear numerical forcing (PARKED)
A long chain, with most of it now refuted; the verdict is in `rt-source-dt-forcing.md`:
- The 3 Msun He box's "pulsation" at shallow ramps is the **box organ-pipe acoustic mode**
  (P = 2L/c_s/n = 107/54/36/27 s; in the B star P2 = 2693 s), pumped by the **operator-split semi-implicit
  two-stream source acting as a delayed thermostat with lag ~ dt**.  `hestar_fecz/conv_fix` at 100/300 with
  the fixed sweep: saturated v_rms = 3.0/2.8/1.7/1.6/0.7/0.5e5 cm/s for dt 0.22/0.12/0.10/0.077/0.032/
  0.028 s -- **monotone in dt and in nothing else** (dx and nquad fall on the same curve; the growth rate
  changes sign; the harmonic selection flips; the explicit source gives Mach 15).  It saturates by 200 s;
  it is not a runaway.  **Not physics.**
- Refuted along the way: the "physical strange mode" lead (`he-star-strange-modes.md` -- a 3 Msun He star
  does sit at the critical mass for Fe-bump strange modes, but this box cannot host them: t_th < 6 s
  everywhere and the walls fix the period); the "handover flux mismatch" lead
  (`rt-shallow-ramp-missplit.md`); the absorbed-field lag (`rt_outer_iter`, 86cc1b0b -- the fixed point
  *diverges*, 0.9 per pass, box goes transonic); the local damping factor (`rt_relax_sub`/`xcrit`/`submax`,
  8507a73e/b1c5a0e5/4cfe92d0, bitwise off, conservative, 1.25x cost -- v_rms still linear in dt to the
  digit); every clip/cap/rescue (11 arms, all counters zero).  The discriminating arm: **two-stream OFF
  (Newton lid) -> v_rms is dt-independent to 0.03 %**, so the pump is inside the two-stream per-cell
  relaxation step -- handover, wall, radial solve and source assembly are all cleared.
- **Verdict**: the pump is the per-cell semi-implicit factor `(1-e^-x)/x` applied to a **non-local**
  exchange (neighbours are damped differently, so nothing cancels -- the same mechanism as the decad388
  handover bug, now cell to cell).  Explicit is unstable; the Jacobi re-sweep diverges; **there is no cheap
  switch**.  The proper fix is an implicit column solve of the radiative balance inside each RK stage
  (Newton-Krylov, or a damped iteration with the sweep as the operator), ~1 week.  It would also make the
  red giant and the dhj time-accurate.
- **The B star tolerates the floor**: `bstar_fecz/dtcheck` (336x8x8, no seed) shows the same forcing with
  v_rms ~ dt^0.7-0.9, giving 1.4e3 cm/s at the production dt of 1.5 s = **0.6 % of v_MLT**, deep Mach
  2e-4, 100x below the FeCZ convective velocities.  Keep `nquad = 2` (nquad 1 is 5x worse).
- He rules that stand: `rad_tau_lo/hi = 100/300` (a shallower ramp is not viable), 5 Msun is
  Eddington-limited (Gamma 0.98) and 8 Msun (Gamma 1.09) must not run closed, 3 Msun is the first target.
  **He production is on hold.**

### 4.5 LHLLD, the Balsara vortex, and the HLLD threshold bug
- **LHLLD aligned with Minoshima & Miyoshi 2021** (ae9f5336): momentum weight one (the base star flux is
  already F(U*); the energy keeps the Batten weight `S_M S_a/(S_a - S_M)`), and
  `chi = min(1, max(c_u)/max(c_f))` with `c_u` the fast-speed formula with `|u|` in place of `c_s` -- a
  **magnetic floor**.  The Mach-1e-6 PPM Alfven checkerboard is gone; lhlld tracks hlld to 0.1 % across
  the whole linear-wave suite (skips removed), and is bitwise = hlld at Mach 5.7.
- **Balsara vortex** (180f7526 pgen + `inputs/mhd/balsara_vortex.athinput`, c1cf759d test): at
  M_Alf 10 the lhlld error is **Mach-independent** (0.010-0.012 vs hlld 0.02 -> 0.13; kinetic energy

## B1. The B-star column pulsation: NUMERICAL, and it is the lambda iteration

`bstar-column-pulsation.md`, `bench/bstar_fecz/puls1d`, 12 arms.  The growing 2150-s acoustic
pulsation that appeared under the whole-column scheme (A2f) is **a scheme artefact, not beta Cephei**:

- the growth rate is **converged in dx** (nx1 168-504 at fixed dt) but **linear in dt**:
  `gamma = 7.64e-5 dt - 1.13e-4 /s`, crossing zero at **dt = 1.48 s = exactly the production step**
  (cfl 0.3 sits on the marginal point, which is why 3-D never showed it);
- the `dt -> 0` limit is a **physically damped** column, `t_damp 8.8e3 s ~ 4 P`;
- `rt_ali_diag = true` **at the production dt** gives `gamma = -6.5e-5` = the small-dt limit.  **ALI is
  the entire difference**; `nquad` and the taper are not;
- the kappa-mechanism sign test **fails** (the mode's energy sits at tau 0.01-20 where
  `dln kappa / dln T < 0` at `t_th = P`), so it is not the Fe bump.

So the whole-column configuration is usable **provided ALI is on**, and ALI is what restores the
physical damping.
