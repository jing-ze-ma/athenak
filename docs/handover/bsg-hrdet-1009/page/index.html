Our radiation: implicit two-moment (E, F) transport with a variable Eddington tensor from short characteristics (120 directions, rotated every 10 steps), not the M1 closure.

What is the same and what is not: IC, domain, opacity tables, outer outflow rule, HLLC, CFL and the absence of a sponge follow the paper. Differences: the 1 % temperature seed (the paper states none), the closed inner wall (theirs: an HLLC-solved zero-mass-flux ghost), the two-moment VET transport instead of 120 transported intensities, the stretched radial grid instead of SMR (the same 0.153 R☉ in 35–55 R☉, 256 angular cells everywhere where theirs has 128 outside the SMR zone), and the 1-D photosphere (our radiative integration of the same profile reaches it at 53.03 R☉; the paper quotes MESA's, about 50–51 R☉). The paper writes rmax = “80 M☉” (a typo for R☉).

Why we seed. Our first reproduction run (repro_4n: no seed, force reference work = full) stayed laterally symmetric to about 10−6 in ρ and T at the Fig. 4 shell, and its kinetic energy fell from the start-up peak to 4×1039 erg by day 7 instead of growing towards the paper's ~1043 erg (panel “KE” below): with no seed, convection would have to grow from round-off. The run shown here adds the 1 % temperature seed and the energy-conserving split work; grid, IC, tables and all other settings are those of repro_4n. The unseeded full run was stopped at day 9.1.
