description: 2026-09-14 LITERATURE - a 3 Msun He star sits AT the critical mass (3-5 Msun, Kiriakidis+96) for Fe-bump STRANGE-MODE instability (growth on the dynamical time; not convective, not classical kappa: needs d kappa/d rho + RADIATION-PRESSURE restoring force in a strongly non-adiabatic layer = our tau 4-64 layer; Saio+98 NAR limit). Nonlinear (Glatzel+99): 6 Msun WR limit cycle, P ~100 s, v ~60 km/s, shocks, tiny L swing, no mass loss - matches our shallow-ramp He column (P 90 s, Mach 0.3). Saturation amplitude is dissipation-sensitive (Grott+05: 100x with viscosity). No 3-D box exists for a 3 Msun He star (gap). CONSEQUENCES: the He 10/100-ramp instability is plausibly PHYSICS (1-D matrix hestar_fecz/instab1d decides); the radiation TAPER must NOT reach the He driving layer (stop at tau ~1 or leave off for the He star).
