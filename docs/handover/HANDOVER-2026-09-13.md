## 2. Findings, most important first

### 2.1 FOFC works on the GPU and replaces the velocity ceiling on the red giant (two machines)
- **FOFC verified on MI300A** (`bench/fofc_gpu`, job 11620418): CPU 48/48 (reproduces orion's numbers
  exactly), HIP Release 48/48; 13 cases differ from CPU only at round-off (div B ~1e-14, vceil/FOFC event
  counts by 0.1%).  The HIP Debug aborts were *not* FOFC: the hydro dt-collapse diagnostic read `x1v` on
  Cartesian grids (fixed b18f3205).  Orion's "build Debug once before trusting a GPU FOFC run" item is DONE.
- **RG_fofc (viper GPU, `hydro/vceil = 0`, `fofc = true`)** reached tlim 9e5 through the 5.7e5 / 5.87e5 /
  6.13e5 death window with dt flat at 30.667 s; orion's CPU twin of the same input did the same.  Two
  independent realizations => FOFC replaces the ceiling on the red giant.  A CPU twin on viper reproduced
  the GPU `rg.log` floor counts row for row, so the red-giant pgen + FOFC run the same on MI300A as on CPU.
  Still missing: a FOFC-off control on the same binary.
- Cube-vertex corner ghost extrapolation went negative in the inflating layer; 2e3ae96e (sign-preserving
  fallback) cut dfloor 1.1e6 -> 320 per interval.  Later shown *not* implicated in any death.

### 2.2 The sp MHD energy excess is the polar-row HLLD->HLLE swap, radial sweep only
Chain: `bench/sp_excess` (B=0 MHD carries the whole excess => it is the module path, not the field) ->
`sp_excess2` (hlle in BOTH modules is bitwise identical => it is the solver) -> `sp_excess3` (the new
`polar_hlle_rows=false` makes MHD == hydro to 6 digits in E/KE/mass, with and without field) ->
`sp_excess4` (**the excess is entirely the x1/radial sweep's swap**: mask 1 alone gives +7.2e35 and 7.4x
KE_h; masks 2 and 4 alone give exactly hydro).  HLLE on the two polar rows dissipates the polar flow and
drives a deep meridional cell.  Every "sp MHD vs hydro" comparison ever made measured this, not the field
(`sp-mhd-energy-excess`, `sp-hydro-vs-mhd-comparison` "field takes 31% of the zonal KE" -- retracted).
**But the swap cannot simply be deleted**: the canonical polar MHD blast (`run/pole_blast`, PROBLEM=
pole_mhd_blast, 256x256x32, added by the same commit 9fb17531 as the swap) with it off drains the FAR
polar row (rho 0.33 -> 0.017) and dt falls geometrically from t~0.045; invisible at 64^2/128^2.  Hence
41d19fce: a per-sweep mask.  Candidate default **mask 6** (theta+phi swapped, radial not) if the blast is
stable with it.  cs has no polar rows and is unaffected.

### 2.3 The red-giant death mechanism: seam-row energy floor -> EOS sentinel (fixed)
`bench/RG_fofc_long/deathmap`: a convective downflow drains a photospheric column 250x; **only on a
panel-seam row** its internal energy collapses (tau ~180 s) to the code floor e/rho = 1.4226e7 erg/g
(T 0.07 K, far below the table's 100 K) -- the across-seam ghost column hit that floor 2.9e4 s earlier --
and the next EOS inversion returns a saturated garbage state T = 2.629264e18 K, cs = 2.66e9, |v| ~ 1e12 in
every collapse cell => dt collapse.  Three of three realizations (clamped, unclamped, restarted) died in
the same column within 10 cycles.  Cause: `EOSTable::SolveLog` returned the *bracket edges*
(10^(ymin-3) = 0.1 K for a pfloor-pinned energy floor, 10^(ymax+3) = 3.16e10 K for an out-of-range or
non-finite target).  **Fixed 5fe7be1c** (clamp both inversions to [ymin,ymax], intercept non-finite
targets, efloor gate >= e(rho,T_min), new `eos_tclamp` event-log column).  Verified: `RG_fofc_long2` ran
1.7e6 -> **tlim 3.0e6** with dt flat where 3/3 unfixed realizations died at 1.7728e6.
Residual: a clamped cell has p = p(rho,T_min) independent of e (e is not raised); `eos_tclamp` makes it visible.
FOFC is irrelevant here (it only fires at r > 3.9e12).

### 2.4 Restarts were not bitwise -- user BCs read primitives (fixed to 1 ulp)
`Driver::InitBoundaryValuesAndPrimitives` calls `ApplyPhysicalBCs` **before** the first `ConToPrim` after a
restart, when `w0` is still zero (the pgen returns early on restart), while a running stage sees the
previous stage's `w0`.  `u0` (including ghosts) is restored bitwise.  Found with a ghost-inclusive
one-cycle continuous-vs-restarted comparison (`bench/RG_fofc_long/bitwise`, recipe in the scripts dir):
active cells identical at the restart cycle, **all** outer radial ghosts differ (rel 5 -> inf), the top
active rows differ one cycle later.  Two bisection rounds showed no physics switch was responsible.
Fixed by 7645550e (red_giant) + e4b318b3 (dhj) + 2667d984 (keep the file's ghosts) + 6740fae3 (`wtemp` in
the restart).  **Residual: ONE outer ghost eint cell at 1 float32 ulp** (the BC writes `w0` ghosts directly,
the restart recomputes `w0 = c2p(u0)` -- an EOS round trip), which the implicit radial conduction spreads to
~1e-6 in one cycle.  Left open.  CPU tabulated-EOS linear wave: bitwise.  **The test suite has no restart
test -- add one.**  Rule for pgens: a user BC must read `u0` (+ `b0`) and convert locally
(`utils/eint_from_cons.hpp`), never `w0`/`wtemp`/`bcc`.

### 2.5 The GPU is deterministic; the collapses are chaotic at the ulp level
Two identical 300-cycle restarts are bitwise identical in the full `hydro_w` dump (11650986).  Earlier
"restarts differ / 5x fewer floors" readings were *different binaries* (hipcc kernel changes move results by
ulps; an unstable episode amplifies them within 300 cycles).  So: the red-giant corona episode at ~1.08e6
and the cs dhj deaths are real instabilities whose survival is decided at the ulp level.  Concretely,
`bench/cs_death_ab`: `cs_mhd_prod3_fofc` died at rot 11.4 and `cs_mhd_prod3_wb` at rot 12.27, but restarts
of both from just before, on the HEAD binary, survived -- **not a regression**; the production
`cs_mhd_prod3` is past rot 82.  Lesson banked: always put `time/dt_min` in the red-giant input (its absence
turned a collapse into a 1e-26 spin).

### 2.6 The cs-vs-sp KE "gap" was sp MHD's artefact
`cs_mhd_prod3_wb` (cs MHD + sp's WB block) tracked plain `cs_mhd_prod3` to 10% in KE/ME/1-ME/E up to its
death at rot 12.3 => WB is not what separates cs from sp.  At rot 12 every cs arm *and* sp hydro sit at
KE 2-3e34; only sp MHD is 1.6e35 with total E rising -- i.e. section 2.2.  The cs deficit claim is withdrawn.

### 2.7 Smaller items
- **RT sweep split shelved**: `rt_chain_ck` down/up sweeps as parallel threads = 4.94 -> 4.39 ms/call
  (1.13x), wall 2.8%.  Latency-bound on the radial recurrence; 2x waves did not buy the predicted 1.45x.
  Patch kept in the scripts dir, not merged (the remaining RT lever is fewer chains = a physics trade).
- **LDS scratch flag** (955c39df): `<hydro>/scratch_level=1` removes the nx1 ceiling for hydro (the red
  giant at nx1=480 aborts at level 0 with "could not find a valid team size").  Bitwise level-0-vs-1 on CPU

- **Corner limiter** (2e3ae96e): sign-preserving fallback in `FillPanelCorners{CC,FC}`; raddiff cs test
  unchanged to 4 digits (a node-range clamp instead broke it, Linf 0.036 -> 0.19, rejected).  Cleared of any
  role in the deaths (clamped and unclamped binaries behave identically).
- Reconstruction note updated: since 4a16f07b cs and sp share the same stretch-aware radial PLM; on cs
