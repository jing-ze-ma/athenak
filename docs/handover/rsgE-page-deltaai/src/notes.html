What the run shows so far (read at t ≈ 16 d, 10-20 evening)

This box is written by hand (src/notes.html) and is not regenerated. The figures, movies and header numbers update automatically.

  • Healthy: no FATAL / DIVERGED / non-converged summaries and no NaN in the log, and no non-finite cells in any dump. dt holds at 1000 s and Picard at 4 per cycle. Energy bookkeeping closes to a few 10−8 of |E0|, and the mass drift is 10−12.
  • Gas temperature checks: Tgas is inverted per cell from (ρ, eint) with the run’s own EOS. The port reproduces the code’s reported μ = 0.648009 and ne/ntot = 0.526021 at the table centre exactly. On the IC column it recovers T to 1.2×10−4 (median 10−6), except at the one row where the column joins the floor gas. In dump 0, Tgas matches the IC column to 1.1×10−4 below 0.99 Rph; above that the in-code IC chain re-builds the atmosphere. Tgas equals Trad to < 7×10−5 everywhere the gas is not floored. The 2000 K floor holds about 5–6 % of the mesh, all of it at r ≳ 2.0–2.2 Rph (floor gas near the outer boundary).
  • Surface steady: Ltop/Lin = 1.000; the median Rph is within 0.01 % of the IC and Teff is 4110 K.
  • Infall above the photosphere is an IC artefact. Dump 0 (after the in-code IC relaxation) has a density inversion: ρ drops to 1.1×10−17 at 1.086 Rph, then jumps to 7.5×10−17 in the next cell (1.0885 Rph) and decays to the 10−17 floor by 1.108 Rph. T is the same on both sides, so pressure rises outward by ×7 and this layer falls inward. Its shell-mean vr grows to −6.7 km/s by ~10 d and stays there, with the layer now at 1.079 Rph and the inversion smoothed out (photosphere movie and band figure). The IC column itself has no inversion: it goes smoothly to 1.0×10−17 at 1.0856 Rph. So the jump comes from the in-code IC chain where the atmosphere meets the floor gas. There is an outflow band of ≈ +0.09 km/s at 0.98–1.04 Rph. The mass involved is negligible.
  • Photosphere noise at the cell scale: in a thin layer at 0.99–1.01 Rph, Tgas fluctuates from cell to cell with an rms of 0.3 % (single cells up to 7.5 %), against 0.03 % just below and above. This is what makes the Teff, Rph and 0.98 / 1.085 Rph maps speckled. Watch whether it grows.
  • Deep flows: the fastest gas sits at 0.05–0.25 Rph (rms vr ≈ 0.1 km/s, single cells about 0.2 km/s at 16 d, growing about linearly), as broad radial lobes. In the plane their peak is near φ ≈ −5°, not on the cube edges. Near 0.2–0.25 Rph, though, the δlog ρ and δT contours show kinks along the ±45° / ±135° diagonals (inner-zoom movie), so the cube edges are starting to imprint. The rings at 0.036, 0.059 and 0.099 Rph are where the lateral cell merging changes factor.
  • Seed still linear in the bulk: at 0.3–0.98 Rph the vr and δTgas maps show the smooth l ≤ 8 Ylm seed pattern, with |vr| of 0.01–0.15 km/s and δT/T ≲ 0.1 %, far below MLT speeds. The sub-grid closure still carries all the convective flux (LMLT/Lin ≈ 1.00).
  • Floors and limiters: the 2000 K floor fires on ~1.4×109 cell-stages per 100 cycles; the density floor and v-ceiling never fire. First-order flux correction fires on every cell of the last two radial cells (the outer boundary).

Caveats

  • Tgas uses a numpy port of the code’s EOS, inverted bilinearly on the code’s own table grid (the code interpolates with bicubic Hermite plus Newton); the difference is ≲ 10−4. Teff and Rph still use Trad and the flux (grey Eddington per column, not a τ integral).
  • Slices and maps show native cells. The planar rings average the two cell rows next to the plane, and the Mollweide maps use one cell per pixel.
  • The energy-budget line is approximate (see the History caption).
  • If a link restarts from a restart file and rewrites a dump index, the page keeps the first reduction of that index (delete out/data/red_NNNNN.npz and the frames to redo it).
