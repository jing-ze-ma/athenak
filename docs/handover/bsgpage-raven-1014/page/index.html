Our radiation: implicit two-moment (E, F) transport with a variable Eddington tensor from short characteristics (120 directions, rotated every 1750 s of model time, averaged over the last 4 rotated sets), not the analytic M1 closure.

What is the same and what is not: IC, domain, opacity tables, the inner zero-contact ghosts, the outer outflow rule, HLLC, CFL, the point-mass gravity without a well-balanced scheme and the absence of a sponge follow the paper. Differences: the two-moment VET transport instead of 120 transported intensities (the one intended difference), the stretched radial grid instead of SMR (the same ~0.156 R☉ in 35–55 R☉, 256 angular cells everywhere where theirs has 128 outside the SMR zone), and the 1-D photosphere (our radiative integration of the same profile reaches it at 53.03 R☉; the paper quotes MESA's, about 50–51 R☉), and our 10−6 temperature seed (see below). The paper writes rmax = “80 M☉” (a typo for R☉).

Seed. The paper states no perturbation. Our unseeded true reproduction on Caltech (truerepro2, 10-09) stayed exactly laterally symmetric to day 6 (KEint entirely radial, the 40.6 R☉ shell uniform to the last bit), so convection could not start from round-off. This run therefore adds a numerical-noise seed: a 10−6 relative temperature perturbation at fixed density in 37.5–45.2 R☉ (16 lateral modes, random signs), with the radiation set in equilibrium with it. It is far below any physical amplitude and only breaks the exact symmetry; the banner above gives the current state.

Definitions and differences

At 20.59 d, Fr is taken at each column's own τR = 1 cell, at 51.52–54.18 R☉ (1st–99th percentile; median 52.93). There Fr/⟨Fr⟩ spans 0.178–3.505 (rms 0.296; Fr < 0 in 0.00 % of the columns), ⟨Teff⟩ = 15517 K and 4π⟨r2Fr⟩ = 5.6×1038 erg/s = 0.997 L.

Why not a fixed layer: above the photosphere the cell-centred radiative flux of an M1/VET run no longer carries the luminosity (its wedge mean falls to 0.1–0.2 L in the thin top), while the energy leaves through the Marshak top face (history Ltop). On the earlier reproduction page a map at the first cell with mean τ̄ < 1 had up to 11 % of its cells with Fr < 0 in porous episodes, where most columns are already thin at that layer; at each column's own τR = 1 the same dump had no negative-flux bands.
