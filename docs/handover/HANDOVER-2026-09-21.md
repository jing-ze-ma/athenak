## 2. The grey M1 radiation module `<rad_m1>` (new, `src/rad_m1/`)

Design, literature findings and every measured number: **`docs/dev/rad_m1_design.md`**
(sections 10-12 are the implementation findings and supersede earlier text).  Results page
with plots: https://claude.ai/artifact/6b5dXN5p9ESoNZkNh6rxs8 (source copy
`bench/m1_stage2/m1_gates_page.html`, data `tests_m1/plots/*.json`).  Gate scripts:
`tests_m1/` (one per gate, `--selftest`).

Commits: 1a 6b9a14f9 (skeleton, free streaming), 1b f271d57d (thick fluxes, implicit
coupling), 1c-A 446918e0 (moving media), 1c-B aea14fc0 (restart, reconstruction, rank and
conservation tests).  What is established:

- Source terms are evaluated in the comoving frame and transformed (= Krumholz et al. 2007
  = AREPO-IDORT eqs 56-57).  T4b (uniform medium, beta*tau_cell = 1e3): gas temperature
  drift exactly 0; the O(v/c)-truncated control runs away at the predicted rate.
- Thick-limit flux `ap_hll` (Berthon/Bloch; alpha on the E-flux only; with PLM the blend
  `alpha F_HLL + (1-alpha) F_diff(cell centres)` with alpha^2 on the reconstructed
  dissipation): diffusion rate 1.00079 / 1.000015 / 1.000000 at tau_cell 10 / 1e3 / 1e6,
  second order, Nyquist mode damped at 0.99996 of the physical rate.  Plain HLL is 21365x
  too fast at tau_cell 1e6 and 78.7x on a clipped top-hat.  `scaled` (the Jiang/IDORT
  modified HLLE in moment form) has a fixed +4 % excess with dc and almost no Nyquist
  damping: option only.
- Moving media need the advective enthalpy-flux split: without it the pulse lags by half
  the advection distance (T4, beta*tau = 14).  `split_vel = recon` default.
- IMEX PD-ARS with the local implicit solve inside BOTH stages (a transport-only stage gives
  a spurious c^2 dt/3 diffusion).  Thick regime stable to a light-speed CFL of 8.
- Root find in T must be rtsafe on the tabulated EOS.  After the coupling writes hydro u0
  the hydro ghosts must be refreshed.
- Restart bitwise; 1/2/4 MeshBlocks and 1/4 ranks bitwise; box G1 bitwise without the
  block after every milestone.
- OPEN: T3b, a 1.4 % flux deficit across a 1e3 opacity jump sitting at tau_cell ~ 1;
  shadow test (T2) not run; T7 (radiative shocks, reference `tests_m1/t7_radshock.py`)
  and T9 belong to the coupled stage.  Code-wide finding: `ParameterInput` records a
  DEFAULTED Real with 6 digits in the restart input, so pgen state derived from a defaulted
  Real is not bitwise restartable.

Reduced speed of light: valid only for chat >> v*tau_max.  Sizing
(`bench/m1_sizing/`): affordable for the He box (tau_bot 322, v_max 8.6e4): cut at tau =
100, chat/c = 2.9e-3 (K = 10), N_sub = 8.8, ~13 node-h per 10 turnovers = the two-stream's
cost.  NOT affordable for the B star (FeCZ at tau 579-3268) nor the global He4 model: those
need implicit transport (stage 3).  Own caution: with chat on the time derivative the
radiation heat capacity is inflated by c/chat unless dE/dt is quasi-static (error ~3/K);
in the radiation-dominated FeCZ run K = 10, 30, 100.

Planck-mean opacities (none existed): `bench/m1_opac/` + `data/stellar_opac/README_planck.md`
