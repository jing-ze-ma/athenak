The He box analysis reported 'box-bounded like the B star' because the k = 1 bin was the largest value of a broad, nearly flat spectrum, and the flux-map lanes coarsened; the maps showed a field of 0.5-1.5 Mm cells and the flux map was the standing box mode.
**Why:** an agent's summary statistic can be technically true and physically wrong; the B-star verdict was solid because it rested on the downdraft count and a sharp k = 1 peak at every depth.
**How to apply:** for scale verdicts require (a) the map viewed by me, (b) the power fraction at k = 1 vs k >= 2 (not argmax), (c) downdraft counts, (d) exclusion of pulsation-dominated maps; state 'borderline' when the spectrum is broad. See [[he-box-m3-staging]], [[bstar-prelim-science-page]].
