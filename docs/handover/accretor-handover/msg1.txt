coordinates: isothermal pressure in the sp / cubed-sphere geometric sources

SrcTermsSphericalPolar{Hydro,MHD}, SrcTermsCurvilinearWB, SrcTermsGnomonicEquiangleImpl
and SrcTermsSphericalPolarCartRows took the pressure as eos.Pressure(d, w0(IEN)), which
for an isothermal EOS reads an energy slot that does not exist (IEN out of range) and
returns (gamma-1)*garbage. With <hydro>/eos = isothermal on a spherical-polar grid the
2p/r radial source was missing: a hydrostatic isothermal atmosphere fell inward at
2 c_s^2/r (ry_per_accretor test a1). Now p = d c_s^2 when !is_ideal; the ideal and
general EOS paths are unchanged (same expression behind the is_ideal branch).
