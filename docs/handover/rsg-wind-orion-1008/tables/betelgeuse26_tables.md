### Chromospheric scale height (H = 0.06 R* at R_min = 1.144 R*)

Eq. (2): H = v_turb^2/(2 g*) (R_min/R*)^2 with v_turb = 19.5 km/s (most probable speed; P_turb = rho v_mp^2/2 = rho v_t^2 with v_t = v_mp/sqrt2 the 1-D rms).

The reduction 0.32/0.73 corresponds to an effective Gamma = 1 - 0.32/0.73 = 0.56; it is applied to the MARCS photosphere only, not to the SEM chromosphere (whose H implies Gamma ~ 0 at v_mp = 19.5).

## Gamma_req (steady wind through the SEM: v(r) = 9 ((0.998 - x^-0.45)/0.998)^1.5 km/s, rho = total SEM rho, P = P_gas + rho v_t^2)

Gamma_req = 1 + r^2/(GM) [v dv/dr + (1/rho) dP_gas/dr + v_t^2 dln rho/dr]. Inside ~1.5 R* the exponential chromosphere carries most of the mass (f_chrom row) and the continuity velocity Mdot/(4 pi r^2 rho_total) falls below the SEM wind-term v: the wind velocity is ill-defined there, Gamma_req is the hydrostatic+turbulent balance (v dv/dr / g < 2e-4).
