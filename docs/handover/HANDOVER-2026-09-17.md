## 2. The B star: `bench/bstar_fecz/prod_w7`

At t = 1.04e5 s = **10.4 turnovers** (1 turnover = 1e4 s).  Entropy seed, k 8-32 window, tau 10-3000.

### 2.1 End of the linear phase

`ln` ratio of the history KE1 per turnover:

| turnover | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| d ln KE1 | +0.94 | +1.07 | +1.16 | +1.21 | +1.24 | +1.23 | +1.13 | +0.73 | +0.03 |

At turnover 10: KE1 = 2.64e34 erg, horizontal 1.05e34 erg.  The roll-over from +1.24 to +0.03 over
three turnovers is the saturation of the linear instability, not a numerical stall.

### 2.2 The maps at dump 10 — one stripe mode, NOT developed convection

`analysis_0916/w7_dump10/bstar` (commands in `analysis_0916/w7_dump10/NOTES.txt`):

- a single coherent **tilted stripe pattern** with four downflow lanes, wavelength ~ L/4 ~ 95 Mm;
- the *same* pattern at tau = 1, 10, 100, 1000 — it is one vertically coherent mode, not a hierarchy;
- rms v_z 0.14 km/s (tau 1) rising to 1.39 km/s (tau 1000); max 6.1 km/s at tau ~ 2900.

So the box is saturating into **one large-scale mode**, not into a turbulent convective spectrum.
Redo the maps and the 3-D viewer at ~15 turnovers, where the stripe should break up nonlinearly.

### 2.3 The f-mode at 10.4 turnovers — flat, and a fitting trap

`analysis_0916/fmode_w7_t10/RESULTS.txt`: the **oscillatory** (2,2) component is flat, **+0.006 per
turnover** over 10.4 turnovers.  Compare +0.004 +- 0.004 at 5.5 turnovers, and **+0.86** with the stale
WB cache.  The cache fix (`wb_cache_every = 0`) therefore holds through saturation.

**CAVEAT, and it is a trap.**  The *naive* band fit over the same data returns **+0.42 per turnover**.
That number is spurious: a near-DC **(4,2)** convective pattern — the stripe mode of §2.2, 69 % of the
non-mean surface power — aliases into the (2,2) band.  **Always split the band into its f_f
(oscillatory) and slow (near-DC) parts before quoting an f-mode growth rate.**  Every f-mode number
from here on must state which part it is.

## 3. The He star: `box_w8` grew, but from 200x too small a seed

`bench/hestar_fecz/box_w8` — velocity seed 1e-3 c_s, `vpert_kmin/kmax` = 4..6, tau 5-50, launched
09-17 00:20.

### 3.1 Onset (`analysis_0916/onset_he_w8/RESULTS.txt`, dumps 1-3)

- The seed was **not** erased this time (w6/w7 entropy seeds were).
- k 4-6 power at i = 84: 1.02e6 -> 1.55e6 -> 2.37e6 (cm/s)^2, i.e. **+0.42 per turnover** in power,
  amplitude e-folding time 4.7 turnovers.  The deep planes grow +0.5-0.6 per turnover, the same rate as
  the successful `box_w4`.
- But the **amplitude is 200x below `box_w4`** at the same turnover, and the dominant power sits in
  k 7-12 and is flat.  At this rate it would take ~25 further turnovers to reach the w4 level.

### 3.2 Cause: the seed wavenumbers are drawn per component

`src/pgen/box_convection.cpp`, ~L2500: `vpert_kmin` / `vpert_kmax` bound **each Cartesian component of
k independently**, not |k|.  So the requested 4..6 actually produces |k| in **5.7-8.5** — above the
box-scale band, which is why most of the power landed in k 7-12 and why the useful k 4-6 content was
only a residue.  This is the same class of mistake as the entropy-seed band in w6/w7.

Maps at dump 3: `analysis_0916/w8_dump3/hestar` — rms v_z 0.04 km/s at tau 1, 0.03 km/s at tau 20-50.
