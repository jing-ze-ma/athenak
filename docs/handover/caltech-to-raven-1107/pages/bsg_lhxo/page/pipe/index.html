What this arm is. The AthenaK deck of the Linhao-exact setup with the values of his athinput.bsg_star.rusty and the kick-fixed Doppler velocity, and no sink or error-limit leak mimic. (1) GM = 19.78 M☉, his floors and his seed threshold (6.15×10−7 g cm−3), one noise deviate per merged group. (2) His SMR angular grid, radiation included: 1282 at 20–35 and 55–80 R☉, 2562 at 35–55 R☉ (merge_lat, rigid momentum). (3) No first-order flux correction, as in his deck. (4) <rad_m1>/doppler_vel = vstar_nbe with he_bc_linhao_fis = iter: the radiation Doppler terms use Athena++'s predicted v*, without the start-up kick of the earlier vstar_n arms. Twin off, ray rotation off. Not matched: the time step. His cfl_rad 0.025 multiplies dt (dt ~4.8 s); this arm uses CFL 0.3 with the radiation-modified signal speed (dt ~100–170 s). We no longer attempt an exact reproduction of his run. Fresh start from t = 0 on 11-06 (14:50 PST); tlim 57.4 d, the paper's end time.
