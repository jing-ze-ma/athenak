Interim page. Our AthenaK run of the 20 M☉ blue supergiant of arXiv:2609.28656 set up to follow Linhao Ma's own Athena++ code (bsg_star.cpp) exactly, except the radiative transfer: branch linhao-prod-1104 (prod-1102 + his IC and inner boundary), binary 26eae921, deck md5 f182f742, 4 H200 on hpc-sm-01-05. Matched to his code: his profile and ±2.5 % isobaric noise, his inner ghosts (profile ρ, P; ghost-1 vr for zero HLLC mass flux), his half-range ghost intensities at the inner face, his outer boundary (outflow ρ ∝ r−2), his exact radial grid (192 base cells + one SMR level on 35–55 R☉ = 256 faces), his 120-angle set as fixed rays. One deliberate fix: the isothermal atmosphere above 52.5 R☉ is hydrostatic in GM/r2 (his code uses g ∝ r+2). Inner-face dissipation in this run: none (user decision for the production: the face is the central average of the ghost and first-cell moments). The companion page shows the test run with his exact face dissipation (athena, taucell 4). Plotted the same way as the paper's Figures 2, 3, 4 and 6, with the published figure beside each one.

What is the same and what is not: domain, opacity tables, the outer outflow rule, HLLC, CFL, the point-mass gravity without a well-balanced scheme and the absence of a sponge follow the paper. Differences: the two-moment VET transport instead of 120 transported intensities (the one intended difference), the stretched radial grid instead of SMR (the same ~0.156 R☉ in 35–55 R☉, 256 angular cells everywhere where theirs has 128 outside the SMR zone), and the 1-D profile top (his profile ends at 52.5 R☉, with his isothermal extension above; the paper quotes MESA's photosphere, about 50–51 R☉), and the IC, inner BC and noise of Linhao Ma's own setup (see below).
