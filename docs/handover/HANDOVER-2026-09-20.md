## 1. The physical result: porosity-driven over-radiation drains the envelope

The 3-D wedge always follows the same history: quiet for ~2 turnovers, convection grows,
then the star radiates more than it is fed, cools at all depths, contracts, and the
envelope between 0.7 R and R empties onto the inner region.  **This happened in every
arm, independent of the MLT closure, of the initial model, of the angular resolution and
of the depth of the inner wall.**

- L_out/L_wedge 1.006 (2.4 turnovers) -> 1.04 (3.0) -> 1.15 (3.5) in wedge9; the low-res
  scout lr1 reaches 1.12/1.21/1.30 at 2.5/3.0/3.5.  tot-E 9.4e40 -> 5.1e40 erg by 4.0.
- KEY SCALE: L_wedge = 4.0776e37 erg/s, L_wedge x turnover = 1.9e41 erg against an
  envelope internal energy ~4.7e40 erg, so **the thermal time of the envelope is ~0.25
  turnover** and a 10 % luminosity imbalance moves half of E_int per turnover.
- CAUSE = radiative POROSITY (tests_r18/r19, poros.py, cmp2s.py).  Horizontal ADI
  diffusion keeps T uniform (rms 0.005-0.05) while rho/<rho> has rms 0.4 (3.0) -> 0.9
  (3.5), min 0.075 max 15.6, because beta = p_gas/p ~ 0.01 gives d ln rho ~ -400 d ln T.
  Each column carries F ~ 1/(kappa rho) and <1/(kappa rho)> >> 1/<kappa rho>.  The
  per-column diffusion flux averaged over the shell reproduces the run's F_2s to 1-5 % at
  all radii and times; the shell-MEAN state misses by 10-40 %.  Porosity factor 1.10-1.49
  over 0.58-0.99 R, corr(F_col, rho) = -0.6..-0.98, kappa_eff/kappa 0.67-0.88 (in the
  Jiang et al. 2015/2018 range), flux limiter inactive, structure resolved (<= 4-cell
  power <= 1.3 % below 0.9 R).
- The horizontal ISOTHERMALITY IS PHYSICAL (tests_r21/chan.py): t_diff across a channel
  1-75 s vs sound crossing 135-2000 s and turnover 1.2e3-1.4e4 s; channels stay optically
  thick across (tau_perp 1.2-7.9 even at the 1 % percentile), so 1-T LTE + diffusion is
  valid.  The one exaggerating ingredient is the strictly RADIAL per-column two-stream:
  mfp/L = 0.13-0.85 in the channels, no oblique leakage into the clumps.  Estimate: 30-50 %
  of the excess removed at 0.93-0.96 R, ~10 % below 0.8 R -> a true excess ~1.07-1.10
  instead of 1.15.  That is the reason for the next step in section 6.
- Arms that do NOT avoid it: no closure at all (wedge11, lr1 -- transition arrives ~0.7
  turnover EARLIER); closure ramped off at 1.2-2.2 turnovers (wedge9e, delayed ~0.8);
  exact radiative-equilibrium ICs (tests_r23 ic_rad_f100, ic_rad_fmass; f100 is flat in
  tot-E to 3e-4 and 10x quieter in momentum for 1.5 turnovers, then loses 17 % by 2.74);
  192^2 vs 96^2 agree to ~1 % in tot-E at 3.0 (the 96^2 scout is faithful);
  inner wall moved from 0.500 R to 0.400 R (tests_r27, section 5).
- END STATE with the thermalising ceiling (tests_r22 lr1t, lr_f100t, 10 turnovers each):
  hst traces go flat at ~6 turnovers, but this is **not** a settled star (retraction
  below): M(r<0.70 R)/M rises 0.13 -> 0.94 (lr_f100t) and 0.29 -> 0.98 (lr1t), only
  0.9-4.7 % of the mass is left above 0.7 R, photosphere / FeCZ / density inversion gone,
  Mach >~ 1 above 0.6 R.  lr_f100t is at least radiatively steady (L_out/L_w 1.010 +-
  0.042 over 8-10, budget closes to 1.5 %); lr1t is garbage after 5.5 turnovers (tau(R)
  0.31, "bursts" of 7-13 L_w from a few hundred near-vacuum pinhole columns).
- Deep-wall arms (tests_r27, 96^2, r_in = 0.400 R, IC layer 90x the envelope mass, thermal
  time 4.6 turnovers): lrd_mlt is quiet to 2 turnovers, L_out/L_w 1.20 (3.0), 1.11 (4.0),
  then ~1.0-1.1; M(0.7-1.0 R) 2.40e25 -> 1.05e25 (4) -> 1.26e24 g (8-8.5, flat, 5 % of the
  original -- the same fraction as the shallow wall).  Better behaved (no evacuation to
  1e-11, no pinhole bursts, outer layers 10x denser, L_out stays ~L) but the drain is the
  same.  **Wall depth was not the issue.**

## 2. Numerics found and fixed (all in 12d3644f, all default off)

- **problem/rt_force_tau_gate** (+ rt_force_tau_lo/hi): the agreed redesign -- EOS
  radiation weight density-only with a very low window (hydro/eos_rad_rho_lo=1e-11,
  rho_hi=1e-10, T gate off) and the radiative FORCE gated by the column optical depth
  inside the two-stream.  It was implemented, verified bitwise when off, IC regenerated
  (tests_r14/convert_ic.py -> ic_he4_neww.txt, ic_he4_tall_neww.txt) and it DID cure the
  tall domain (Btallg: dt ~4 s and mass constant to 8 digits to 2.2 turnovers, against
  dt 0.54 s from the start with the old physics).  **But it is now switched off for good**:
  the 1-D discriminator tests_r15 G0-G7 showed an anomalous inward acceleration of
  -2e4 cm/s^2 (3 g) in the ONE cell with 0 < w < 1, present only with the gate on -- the
  centred +grad(w P_rad) does not cancel the Riemann solver's -grad(w P_rad) at the window
  edge.  That cell is where wedge6 died (density cliff at 1.021-1.026 R, 2.49 turnovers).
  The winning arm G7 = **problem/rt_rad_force=false + rt_force_tau_gate=false**: dt rises
  to 4.9 s, KEr/momentum within 5 %/3 % of the reference, no cliff, floor gas quiet.
- **hydro/rad_gate_rho must be 0.0 in the tall domain.**  RadGate(rho) is not just a
  transverse-diffusion gate: it multiplies the two-stream opacity kc_g AND the conduction
  kappa.  Left at the old 4.97e-10 it kills dt (0.07 s by 0.6 turnover); at 1e-11 the floor
  atmosphere has ZERO opacity and cannot cool (hot cells at 1.1e5 K and rising).  With 0.0
  the floor gas relaxes to radiative equilibrium, 3.54e4 K.  Un-gated transverse diffusion
  is safe because the conduction flux limiter (F_free = 4 sigma T^4) is always on.
- **Strang vs unsplit is an MLT closure SEED problem, not a scheme problem** (tests_r16).
  Under rt_strang=false the first closure call happens before the in-stage two-stream
  sweep, so rt_face_flux_ready() is false, F_2s = 0 in dfc = F_req - F_rad - F_res - F_2s,
  min(F_mlt, dfc) latches the MLT amplitude with no relaxation, and mlt_relax_time = 1e4 s
  then freezes 2.05x too much subgrid flux.  mlt_alpha=0 makes the two modes agree to
  0.04 %.  **This retracts the 09-18/09-19 verdict "rt_strang=false diverges / unsplit is
  inconsistent".**  NOT fixed in code (fix = build the face flux before the first closure
  call, or do not latch on a call without it).  Side finding: fmlt1d relaxes once per RK
  STAGE, so mlt_relax_time is effectively 1/1.5 of nominal.
- **The deep shell-density zigzag was the split-deposit loop** (tests_r15/r17).  The first
  quantity to alternate is F_2s, not T: F_used^(n+1) = F_req - F_2s^n with F_2s^n =
  F_2s^eq - a F_used^n, a = theta/(1+theta) -- a grid-scale feedback whose gain grows with
  dt; the max(0,.) rectifier makes the deposit one-signed and rho ratchets because
  p = p(T) to 2 %.  It killed wedge8 at cfl 0.3 (rho at 0.746 R 2.29e-9 -> 5.8e-10 over
  1.9 turnovers).  FIX = **problem/mlt_split_sync** with **problem/mlt_sync_passes=4**:
  [1 2 1]/4 passes over the faces of the closure TARGET before relaxation (conservative,
  walls mirrored).  1-D: zz(rho) 0.618 -> 0.025 at cfl 0.3, momentum/KEr within 4 %/8 % of
  the dt-converged reference, 3-6-face F_2s power 0.27 -> 0.145.  Verified in 3-D (wedge9).
  Tried and failed: re-forming the deficit from the sweep just done (blows up), dropping
  the rectifier.  **problem/mlt_relax_down_time is HARMFUL in 1-D -- leave it off.**
- **With the sync fix, cfl 0.3 is usable** (wedge9: dt 6.3 s to 2.5 turnovers, 4.85 s at
  3.0, against 1.35 s at cfl 0.15 without it); all current low-res arms run cfl 0.3.
- **The MLT closure costs ~85 % of the runtime**: the same 144x192^2 wedge runs at 4.1 /
  1.99 / 1.05 s per cycle on 1/2/4 nodes with the closure, and 0.56 s/cycle on ONE node
  without it (shell-mean reductions per face per stage suspected, not profiled).  Since the
  closure carries <= 3 % of F_req once convection is resolved and caused most of the
  numerics above, the recommendation given to the user was to drop it -- which is what the
  current arms do (problem/mlt_alpha=0.0).
- **The velocity ceiling deleted energy** (tests_r24): hydro/vceil = 1e8 in
  src/eos/general_c2p_hyd.hpp rescaled the momentum and did u.e -= (1-fs^2) e_k, i.e.
  clipped kinetic energy was thrown away, 9.7e40 erg by 4.75 turnovers in lr1.  FIX =
  **hydro/vceil_thermalise=true** (athena_v24), applied at all six ceiling sites
  (general/ideal hyd+mhd c2p, gnomonic raise-vel hyd+mhd), new event-log column vceil_de.
  Every He4 run past the onset of eos_vceil events without it is untrustworthy from there
  on (wedge3/4/5/7/9/10/11, lr1, lr_f100, lr_fmass).
- Two diagnostics worth knowing: hst tot-E = int(eint + KE + rho Phi) with
  Phi = GM(1/r_in - 1/r), so the "constant +4.1e40 offset" is the potential term (use
  differences only); and the dfloor counts in he4.log are ghost-layer constants scaled by
  the log interval -- **not a signal**.
- wedge7's dt erosion is physical, not a bug (tests_r17x/dt3d.py reproduces the run's dt
  to 0.1-1.5 %): dt is set at r/R 0.60-0.64 in the PHI direction by the sound speed in
  under-dense cells, c_s ~ rho^-1/2 at p_gas/p = 0.009 -- the same beta lever arm as the
  porosity.

## 6. Open questions and the agreed next step

**NEXT STEP (agreed with the user):** add a default-off option to the two-stream column
sweep that lets each column see kappa*rho averaged HORIZONTALLY over roughly one photon
mean free path -- a cheap stand-in for oblique transport, which the strictly radial
per-column solver has none of (mfp/L 0.13-0.85 in the channels).  Test it at 96^2 with the
section-4 configuration.  It decides the central open question: **is the collapse physical
(a near-Eddington envelope that cannot be in 1-D hydrostatic equilibrium) or an artefact of
the radial-only scheme?**  Expected size of the correction if the tests_r21 estimate holds:
excess 1.15 -> 1.07-1.10, which is comparable to the imbalance that drives the drain.

Other open items:
- Is there a steady porous state at all, or does the model only have the collapsed
  thin-envelope answer?  lr_f100t is radiatively steady at 8-10 turnovers but with 95 % of
  the mass inside 0.7 R.
- The MLT closure seed (rt_face_flux_ready on the first call) is diagnosed, not fixed.
- With rt_rad_force=false and the EOS window at 1e-11..1e-10, gas above the window feels
  no radiative force at all (Gamma = 0 there).  It behaves well, but this is an
  approximation nobody has justified; the per-cell EOS weight w(tau) idea (scoped: ~20
  load-bearing sites, 1-2 days, needs w over all ghosts and a one-cycle lag) is the
  principled replacement.
- Whether cfl 0.3 is safe with NO closure at all was never measured separately; it was
  established for the closure + mlt_split_sync combination.
- Resolution: channel autocorrelation is 3.2-7 cells at 192^2 and 1.3-3.6 at 96^2;
  30-68 % of the outer density variance at 96^2 sits at <= 4 cells.  Do not build a 192^2
  IC from a 96^2 collapsed state.
- Literature to lean on: Jiang et al. 2015 and 2018 (arXiv:1809.10187, global wedges with
  a radiative no-MLT IC relaxed through a violent transient, domain to several R),
  Schultz et al. 2022 (arXiv:2110.13944), Goldberg et al. 2022, Anders-Brown-Oishi 2018
  accelerated evolution (arXiv:1807.06687).
