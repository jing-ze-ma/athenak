drift of the solve.  A tighter conservation statement needs a double-precision full-field
dump and was not made.

================================================================================
PASS COUNTS -- what they scale with
================================================================================
The cost parameter of the line-Jacobi outer iteration is the TRANSVERSE DIFFUSION CFL,
nu_t = c dt / (3 tau_cell dx), not c dt/dx:

  tau_cell   implicit_cfl    2-D passes    3-D passes
     1e3          1              3             4
     1e3        1e2              8            10
     1e3        1e4            131             -
      10          1              8            10
      10        1e2            131             -

i.e. passes ~ 1 + O(nu_t) once nu_t > 1.  This is why implicit_maxit defaults to 200
under transport = implicit.  A Krylov wrapper (implicit_solver = bicgstab) is the
intended remedy and is NOT implemented.
