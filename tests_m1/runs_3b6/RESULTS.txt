THE HEADLINE: THE TRANSVERSE COUPLING IS NOT THE BUG
================================================================================
Phase D named "the transverse radiative force without a well-balanced reference" as the
leading suspect for the seeded slab's 0.157 /s KE_2 growth.  The new T10 wave gate
measures exactly that coupling -- the x2/x3 momentum deposit, the bmom_half rule, the
derived F_2 = face mean + A_2 E, the work term with transverse components, the
upwinding of A_2 and the velocity-dependent E0 correction -- against the x1 path that
3a/3b already gate, and finds NO difference: the same wave rotated by 90 degrees
travels at the same speed and damps at the same rate to 1.2e-7 / 1.9e-5, and for two
arms that share a mesh shape the agreement is BITWISE.  No bug was found and none was
fixed; what changed in src/ is diagnostic (see "Source changes" below).

    transverse face flux is no longer a pure diffusion.

turns a 1e-3 relative E asymmetry into a super-luminal face flux; the free surface and
the imposed-flux bottom; the seed itself.  All of those are in the He slab.

  * KE_1 GROWS AT 0.16-0.19 /s IN EVERY ARM, 80-90x the convective rate -- including

  * The TRANSVERSE force is an AMPLIFIER, not the driver: switching it off cuts

  * s_noheat (the FORCE alone) is as bad as the base run or worse (KE_2 4.6e29), so the

  * s_noforce is NOT a clean control: with force_reference = wb_arad the well-balanced
    gravity source subtracts rho a_rad_ref from the momentum on the assumption that the
    radiation puts it back, so removing the force leaves the slab out of hydrostatic
    balance by the whole radiative support.  It is reported because its KE_1 growth rate
    (0.160 /s) is within 12 % of the base one, i.e. the growth survives the removal.

WHAT REMAINS (the phase-E "not done" list).  The seeded slab is still unphysical and the
cause is NOT in the transverse gas coupling, which T10 now gates.  The remaining
differences between T10 and the slab, in the order they should be attacked:
  (1) the optically THIN top, where the face-eliminated CENTRAL transverse flux has no
      HLL dissipation and c^2 dt/dx ~ c x 7e3: a 1e-3 relative E asymmetry between
      horizontally adjacent cells gives a super-luminal face flux, and the positivity
      fallback fires on 1388 of 1393 steps in the base arm.  An asymptotic-preserving /
      HLL-blended transverse face flux (already on the phase-D "not done" list) is the
      obvious next build, and T10 at tau_lambda = 1e2 is the regime where it can be
      gated;
  (2) the well-balanced reference: force_reference = wb_arad subtracts rho a_rad_ref(z)
      from the x1 momentum ONLY, and there is no equivalent horizontal balance -- a
      2-D static-slab gate with a horizontal perturbation of rho alone would say whether
      the x2 force is well balanced at rest;
  (3) the imposed-flux bottom and the Marshak free surface in 2-D (T10 is periodic);
  (4) the seed itself: whether the same seed in the two-stream production run and in the
      M1 slab excite the same modes has not been compared.
