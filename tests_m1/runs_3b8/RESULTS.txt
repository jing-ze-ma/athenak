WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
Two new <rad_m1> parameters, both inert at their defaults:

  implicit_trans_limit = none | lp      (default none)
  implicit_trans_fmax  = <Real>         (default 1.0)

Under `lp` every TRANSVERSE (x2/x3) face of the face-eliminated implicit transport gets
a lagged "limiter opacity"

  klim_f  = |G_f| / (phi_f E_f),     G_f = gr_f + off_f  (the face pressure divergence
                                     the flux kernel already forms, lagged iterate)
  E_f     = (E_L + E_R)/2 of the lagged iterate, floored
  phi_f   = fmax * sqrt(max(1 - f1_f^2, 0.01)),  f1_f = face mean of the lagged cell
                                     F1/(c E), clipped to [-1,1]
  theta_f = 1/(1 + c dt (kt_f + klim_f))

so that in steady state |F_f| = c|G_f|/(kt_f + |G_f|/(phi_f E_f)) <= c phi_f E_f, while
where R = |G_f|/(kt_f E_f) << 1 (every optically thick face) the change is O(R).  It is
a Levermore-Pomraning limiter written as an opacity, so that it enters the ALREADY
linear face elimination.  klim only SHRINKS theta, so the 7-point M-matrix property is
preserved.

theta is computed ONCE per evaluation into the new face arrays thx2/thx3
(RadiationM1::ImplicitTransTheta) and READ by: the x2/x3 face-flux kernels, the
cell-terms kernel (TDIA, CJM/CJP/CKM/CKP), ImplicitOffDiagOp (hence the BiCGStab
operator) and the post-solve reconstruction -- they cannot drift apart.  klim follows
implicit_closure_lag: frozen at the entry state for the whole step under `step`,
recomputed each Picard pass under `pass`.

  * KE_2 (the HORIZONTAL kinetic energy, the unphysical part) falls 1.8 decades at
    fmax = 1 and 2.4 decades at fmax = 0.5.  KE_1 falls by one decade.
  * F1top/Fin, i.e. whether the slab still transports its imposed flux out of the top,
    goes from 0.07-0.66 to 0.82-0.95 (fmax 1) and 0.95-1.00 (fmax 0.5).  A7_implx1
    (no transverse flux at all) is the quiet reference at 1.000; fmax = 0.5 is on it.
  * The Picard loop is healthy again: 7.6-7.9 mean, 9-10 max, ZERO non-converged, against
    12.5 mean / 200 max and one non-converged step with the limiter off.
