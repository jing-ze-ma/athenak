THE HEADLINE
================================================================================
The 0.16-0.19 /s is NOT a growth rate of a mode -- it is the slope of a ONE-OFF RAMP
that saturates by t ~ 5 s in every arm (KE_1 in s_base: 1.4e26 at 1 s, 2.7e28 at 5 s,
4.0e28 at 10 s, 6.9e28 at 200 s; d ln KE/dt over 20-200 s is 0.0011 /s).  What the
question really is, is WHY the ramp stops at KE ~ 1e29 (v_rms ~ 7 v_MLT, max|v| 740
v_MLT) instead of at the hydrostatic noise floor.

THE ANSWER IS H3, and it is not close:
  * 99.0-99.5 % of the horizontally averaged v^2 of the seeded M1 slab sits at
    tau < 1, and the peak is at tau = 0.004-0.08, i.e. in the four or five optically
    THINNEST cells -- NOT in the seed window (tau 5-50) and NOT in the layer H1 makes
    unstable (tau 14-45).
  * Cutting the box at tau = 1 (A5) removes it: KE_2 falls by 7.6 decades, F1top/Fin
    goes from 0.394 to 0.99970, max|v| from 740 to 14 v_MLT, and the positivity fallback
    from 1388 of 1393 steps to ZERO of 1240.
  * Hydro alone with the SAME frozen reference acceleration and the SAME seed (A1)
    reaches max|v| = 0.21 v_MLT in 200 s.  H1 and H2 are dead as explanations of the
    0.16 /s.

seeded M1 slab does the OPPOSITE: 99 % above tau = 1, peak at tau = 0.004-0.08, nothing
at all in the H1 band.  The two are not the same phenomenon.


================================================================================
VERDICT
================================================================================
H3.  The 0.16-0.19 /s ramp and the 1e29 plateau of the seeded 2-D He slab are made in
the OPTICALLY THIN TOP (tau < 0.1), by the multi-D implicit transport there, and by
nothing else on the list.  The mechanism is the one phase E already named as suspect
(1): the face-eliminated CENTRAL transverse flux has no HLL dissipation, and at
c^2 dt/dx ~ c x 7e3 a per-mille horizontal asymmetry in E -- which the seed's own
acoustic adjustment delivers to the surface within a sound crossing, ~5 s, exactly the
ramp time -- becomes a super-luminal transverse face flux; the positivity fallback then
fires on 1388 of 1393 steps, i.e. after its first pass nearly every step runs in the
22 %-error `none` mode.  Move the top face to tau = 1 and every symptom disappears at
once (KE_2 -7.6 decades, F1top/Fin 0.394 -> 0.9997, fallbacks 1388 -> 0, Picard 12.75
-> 4.18, NON-CONVERGED 9 -> 0).

H1 is REAL BUT NOT THE DRIVER, and this still matters for the formulation.  A frozen
reference acceleration is not a neutral bookkeeping device: presenting the hydro with
g_eff = g - a_rad_ref and a GAS-ONLY equation of state makes the stratification
Schwarzschild-unstable over tau 14-45 at up to 0.0163 /s, against a true mixture
instability of 0.027 /s over tau 4-64 -- the split formulation gets the sign right and
the layer roughly right, but it is a DIFFERENT unstable layer with a DIFFERENT rate,
and it is only the residual force plus the thermal coupling that can restore the
mixture's buoyancy.  At the level this 200 s experiment can see (A1 saturates at 0.21
v_MLT, 25 e-foldings short of anything), the frozen reference is harmless; over a
turnover time it is not obviously so, and a longer A1 (5-10 turnovers, hydro only) is
the cheap way to find out -- it costs 20 s of CPU per 200 s of model.

H2 is REFUTED as a driver: A1 and A3_cfl are identical to four digits, the ramp is
amplitude-linear, and the well-balanced effective potential is measurably BETTER than
the alternatives (A1 0.21 v_MLT against A4_nophi 14.5 and A4_norad 153).
H4 is REFUTED: A6 tracks A0.

WHAT TO TEST NEXT (in this order)
 1. The asymptotic-preserving / HLL-blended TRANSVERSE face flux for the implicit
    operator -- already the phase-E "next build".  It now has a gate at each end: T10 at
    tau_lambda = 1e2 for correctness, and A5 vs A0 here for the cure (the fix must make
    the FULL 84-cell slab behave like the 65-cell one).
 2. A positivity-preserving 9-/19-point operator, so the fallback to `none` stops being
    the normal mode of the thin top.
 3. Independently of 1-2: a longer HYDRO-ONLY A1 (5-10 turnovers) to measure what the
    frozen a_rad_ref actually does to the stratification on a convective timescale, and
    then, if it does anything, the two candidate cures -- refreshing a_rad_ref every N
    steps from the horizontal mean of kappa_R F/c, or dropping the reference and making
    the well-balanced x1 scheme aware of the instantaneous radiative force.
 4. NOT a priority: the seed.  It is exonerated.
