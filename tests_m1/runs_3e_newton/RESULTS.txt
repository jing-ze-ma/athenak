WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
Four new <rad_m1> parameters, all inert at their defaults:

  implicit_accel          = none | anderson    (default none)
  implicit_anderson_m     = 5                  (history depth, 1..10)
  implicit_anderson_beta  = 1.0                (mixing, (0,1])
  implicit_anderson_start = 1                  (first 0-based accelerated pass)

THE FIXED-POINT VECTOR.  One Picard pass is the map x -> G(x) with

  x = (E, F_1, F_2, F_3)   per cell

-- the four quantities the TOP of a pass rebuilds the closure from (rf = |F|/(cE) gives
chi, n = F/|F|, hence D_ab, the enthalpy coefficients a_d and de0).  Nothing else a pass
reads is independent state: the face fluxes f0x1/f0x2/f0x3 are recomputed from E at the
end of every pass, the velocities and opacities are frozen over the step, and T' is
slaved to E' by the scalar root find.  Accelerating E alone would leave the closure built
from an unaccelerated F.

SCALING.  Each cell is divided by its OWN energy scale S = max(E^n, e_floor), frozen for
the whole step (so the least-squares problem stays linear across passes), and the flux
components by c S:

  x = ( E/S , F_1/(cS) , F_2/(cS) , F_3/(cS) )

i.e. the Anderson residual is the RELATIVE fixed-point residual cell by cell, so E and
F/c are commensurate and the optically thin top -- where E is ~10 decades below the base
and where the divergence lives -- carries the same weight as the base.

THE ALGORITHM (Walker & Ni 2011).  With g_k = G(x_k) - x_k and the histories
dX_t = x_{t+1} - x_t, dG_t = g_{t+1} - g_t of the last m passes,

  gamma   = argmin || g_k - dG gamma ||_2
  x_{k+1} = x_k + beta g_k - (dX + beta dG) gamma .

The least squares is solved on the HOST from the normal equations
(dG^T dG + lambda I) gamma = dG^T g_k with lambda = 1e-10 tr(dG^T dG)/m (Tikhonov) by
Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting.  Every inner product is a GLOBAL sum: one
MPI_Allreduce of m+1 doubles per history row (the new M1GlobalSumArr), so the answer is
decomposition-independent (only serial was tested).  The histories are device Views in a
ring buffer, allocated only when the acceleration is on.

SAFEGUARDS
  * the accelerated iterate has the realizability limits re-applied (E >= e_floor,
    |F| <= c E) before it becomes the next lagged state;
  * if ||g_k|| grows by more than 10x over the previous pass the history is DROPPED and
    that pass falls back to plain Picard (counted as a "history restart");
  * the LAST pass of a step is never accelerated, so the state a step ends on is the one
    the stored face fluxes were built from;
  * the convergence test is UNCHANGED (implicit_tol on |dE|/E plus the true 7-point
    linear residual against implicit_lin_tol), measured on the UNACCELERATED map, which
    is the fixed-point residual itself.

The worst cell is ALWAYS in the optically thin top: the depth index i (active range
i = 3..86, 84 cells) is distributed over

    m=5 :  i = 75(1) 77(8) 78(4) 79(9) 80(3) 81(9) 82(2) 83(7) 84(2) 85(7) 86(4)

and NEVER below i = 75, i.e. never outside the top 12 cells (tau < ~0.1), exactly the
layer where c dt/dx ~ 7e3 and where |F_2|/(cE) sits on the realizability clip of 1.
The optically thick column below converges to round-off in a handful of passes, as it
always has.

(iv) WHAT ANDERSON DOES BUY.  It is not useless: it reduces the amount of work and the
size of the residual monotonically as the map is made better conditioned --
7-point linear residual 1.0e3 (none) -> 1.2e3 (m=5) -> 1.5e2 (m=10, beta=0.5) ->
7.2e1 (m=5 + lp) -- and the lp arm keeps dt at its initial 0.1613 s (19 steps to t=3
against 72 with no acceleration).  On the STATIC slab (N-b) it is a clean 3.5x.  But on
the seeded slab it moves the stall, it does not remove it.

HONEST VERDICT.  Anderson acceleration of the per-pass lagged closure does NOT make the
nonlinear closure converge inside the implicit step on this problem.  The fixed-point
map in the optically thin top is not merely slowly contracting -- it has no fixed point
that the Picard/Anderson iterate can reach while the transverse pressure divergence is
evaluated at the lagged closure and the positivity fallback fires on 100 % of the steps.
What this rules OUT is that the trouble is only the RATE of the outer iteration; what it
points at is the operator itself (the 9-point off-diagonal form is not an M-matrix and
min E goes to -3e6..-3e7 on every step, so the linear solve is handing the closure a
non-realizable state each pass).  A positivity-preserving / asymptotic-preserving
transverse face flux, or a true Newton step on the coupled (E,F) system with the closure
Jacobian, is what the evidence now asks for -- not more acceleration of this map.
