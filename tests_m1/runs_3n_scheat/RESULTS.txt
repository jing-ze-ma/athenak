WHAT WAS BUILT (rad_sc_heat.cpp; default path untouched, all keys read only on
transport = sc_heating)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per hydro step, operator split after the hydro integrator, on the implicit-M1
infrastructure (work arrays, the SC sweep incl. the multi-block/MPI band exchange):
 (0) T^n, rho e^n from hydro; chi = rho(kF + ks); eps = min(kP/chi, 1) (same thermal
     fraction as the VET source); approximate lambda operator L* = sum_r w_r w0_r
     (the SC weight of the cell's own source, per ray; boundary layers incl. the
     1.5 S_0 face extrapolation).
 (1) one sweep of S = eps B + (1 - eps) J  -> J_fs, H.  Bottom: I = S_face + 3 F_in mu/c
     (the Milne form; F_in = implicit_flux_x1min), top vacuum.
 (2) ALI + Newton on T per cell: J' = J_fs + L*(S' - S), rho e(T') = rho e^n + dt c eps
     chi (J' - B') -> the module's bracketed Newton (M1ImplTemperature).
 (3) hand-over + x1 line solve (sc_diff_tau = 3): see choice 2 below.
 (4) force dm = dt chi H (diffusion flux in the hand-over), minus dt rho a_rad_ref
     (force_reference = wb_arad), work of the full force: the implicit-M1 write-back
     convention.  u0(E, F) := (J', c H) for the history/outputs only.
Keys: sc_ali_maxit/sc_ali_tol (lambda iterations), sc_lstar, sc_diff_tau, sc_ali_line,
sc_strang, sc_rad_cv, sc_force, and diagnostics sc_ali_tri, sc_diag, sc_dump,
sc_re_iter (gate 3).  Recommended: sc_diff_tau = 3, everything else default.
Limits: hand-over / line solve need ONE MeshBlock along x1 (fatal otherwise; x2/x3
splits fine, 3-D box = 4 blocks OK); without the hand-over any decomposition works.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHYSICS CHOICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Stiffness in the thin top.  Explicit Q would need dt < t_cool ~ e/(4 c chi_th B),
   estimated ~0.01 s in the top cell, ~5e-4 s at tau ~ 0.3 (dt = 0.16 s).  ALI-implicit
   local update has no such limit: thin cells (L* -> 0) have implicit emission against
   the frozen J; thick cells get the coupling c chi (1 - L*), the diffusion rate.  No
   Newton bracket failure in any run (0 no-bracket cells).  Lambda iterations: the
   first-sweep residual max|S' - S|/S' is largest in the TOP (run-mean per layer
   1.8e-3 in the top cell, 1-3e-4 below tau ~ 1; run-max 5e-2 at the top in the first
   steps, the IC adjustment) and shrinks with dt: step mean 1.9e-3 / 4.3e-4 / 2.2e-4 at
   cfl 0.3 / 0.15 / 0.075 (FL logs).  To reach 1e-4 needs > 4 sweeps in 877 of 1240
   steps (2-D, arm ST3_K4 with the diagnostic tridiagonal ALI); on the GPU (diagonal
   ALI) mean 2.96 sweeps (2-D), 4 of max 4 in 60/60 steps (3-D).
   BUT more than one sweep per step is harmful here: with hand-over + force, 2 or 8
   sweeps drive horizontal KE growth everywhere (KE2 at 100 s: 2.6e25 (K2), 1.2e26 (K8)
   vs 1.4e23 (K1); rate ~0.04/s), with L* = 0 as well (K2_nol 2.5e25); not with the
   hand-over off (K2_noh 2.9e23) or the force off (K2_nof 1.3e22).  Default: 1 sweep.
   The x1-TRIDIAGONAL ALI (sc_ali_tri, diagnostic) converges RE 14x faster (1881 vs
   26453 sweeps) but diverges in the dynamics: without the hand-over dt collapsed at
   cycle 10 (run ST0), with the hand-over at tau 30 and 2 sweeps at cycle 8 (K2_d30):
   it assumes full vertical coupling for every horizontal Fourier mode and
   over-corrects the rest.
2. Deep interior.  The J - S form alone CANNOT pin the flux: gate 3 below, the discrete
   radiative equilibrium of the pure SC-ALI carries 0.958 F_in (T 1.1 % low) at every
   nmu (2, 4, 8) -- a J/S residual of 1.7e-5 in the bottom cell is amplified by the
   thick column; refining 2x / 4x (python replica of the kernel) gives 0.9967 / 1.0000.
   In the 2-D slab without the hand-over (FN) the SC flux at 160 s spans 0.92-1.09 F_in
   and the deep T drifts 5 % from the M1 run by 100 s: the one-sweep Jacobi lag makes
   the deep thermal relaxation ~2 dt c/(3 chi dx^2) 4B/e ~ 1e3-1e4 times too slow
   (tau_cell ~ 2, E/e_gas ~ 1.6 there).  An EXPLICIT flux-divergence form -c div H
   is conservative but violates the same diffusion limit (dt/t_diff,cell ~ 1e3).
   Adopted: below tau0 = 3 (weight 0 -> 1 over tau 3 -> 6) the heating is the
   conservative diffusion flux divergence sum_f c/(3 chi_f dx^2)(B_nb - B') with F_in
   imposed at the bottom face, solved IMPLICITLY along x1 (Thomas per column, EOS
   linearised, 2 passes, transverse neighbours lagged); the force there is the mean of
   the two face diffusion fluxes.  "Noise" with/without (step-1000 dumps, deep layers
   0-40): F_sc/F_in in [0.92, 1.08] vs [0.993, 1.028]; gate 3 flux 0.958 vs 0.9991.
3. Radiation energy and pressure are NOT evolved.  The slab keeps eos_radiation =
   false (gas-only EOS): the radiation pressure enters only through the force chi F/c,
   which in the diffusion limit IS -grad(aT^4/3); putting aT^4 in the EOS as well would
   count it twice.  What is lost vs M1: the radiation heat capacity 4aT^3 and inertia,
   and the radiation enthalpy flux (4/3) E v.  At t = 0 (F_ref profile) E/e_gas and
   P_rad/P_gas = 1.64/0.55 (bottom), 1.02/0.54 (layer 20), 0.36/0.27 (40), 0.12/0.13
   (60), 0.81/1.22 (top cell): not small.  Tested the first with sc_rad_cv (LTE aT^4 in
   the hand-over heat capacity): no measurable change in this 200 s comparison
   (FSR_cfl015 vs FS_cfl015: KE@100 2.84e25 vs 2.98e25, deep T err 2.96e-3 vs 3.25e-3).
   The missing enthalpy advection is untested; it matters once convection carries flux.
4. Radiation force from the SC flux, dm = dt chi H, residual to a_rad_ref (Phi_eff
   carries the reference).  At t = 0 the SC flux is within 0.3 % of F_in in layers
   0-40, up to 4.5 % high in 41-65 and 5-9 % in the top 18 (the M1 IC is not the SC
   equilibrium there): the top relaxes to the Hopf profile (2-3 % cooler than the M1
   Marshak top) and rings the column (V1mid up to 4e4 cm/s in the first 30 s at
   cfl 0.3).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * The SC heating path works only WITH the implicit diffusion hand-over below tau ~ 3:
   the J - S form cannot fix the flux in the thick interior (4 % in RE) and its
   Jacobi lag freezes the deep thermal relaxation.  With it: stable, RE exact to 1.6e-3
   in T, the 200 s slab within 3e-3 of M1 in T below the top, a more correct top.
 * It is 2.6-3.6x cheaper per step on the GPU, but at the production step its dynamics
   are dt-dependent (split-lag ratio 1.29-1.32 per halving; M1 0.97), Strang = Lie at
   dt/2, and extra lambda iterations destabilise horizontal modes.  A production SC
   heating needs the source inside the RK stages (IMEX), which is expected to cost
   about Strang (2 sweeps per step) -- i.e. ~1.7x (2-D) / 2.5x (3-D) cheaper than M1
   per step IF it removes the dt dependence; untested.
 * Physics not in the SC path: radiation inertia and enthalpy advection (E/e_gas up to
   1.6 here); the heat-capacity part made no difference in 200 s.
