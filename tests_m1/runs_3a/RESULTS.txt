I6  FREE STREAMING and the thin limit -- THE HONEST NUMBER
----------------------------------------------------------
pulse1d, f = 1, 128 cells, one box crossing, no opacity; the exact answer is the initial
condition.  Peak-to-trough amplitude of E, final/initial:
   explicit          0.953
   implicit CFL 0.4  0.369   CFL 1  0.319   CFL 10  0.285   CFL 100  0.076
The implicit scheme does NOT propagate a free-streaming front: it damps it heavily even
at CFL < 1, because the face-eliminated operator is a backward-Euler discretisation of
the wave system with no upwinding.  This is the intended (and stated) behaviour of the
scheme in the thin limit, and it is why `transport = explicit` stays the default.  It
also means implicit_x1 must not be used for beams, shadows or any thin-region dynamics.

(chi(f)/f = q0 + tau).  There is no test problem generator for a static plane-parallel
column with fixed gas in this tree, and writing one was traded for I8, which is the same
physics with the real stellar column.  See "gaps".

ANSWER: the oscillation does NOT grow.  At the true speed of light, with no sub-cycling
and one backward-Euler solve per hydro step, the column settles within ~200 s and then
DECAYS (gamma = -2.9e-5/s); the drifts at t = 975 s are SMALLER than at t = 400 s
(drho 1.9e-2 vs 5.8e-2).  The residual max|v1| = 10.3 v_MLT is steady and sits in the
BOTTOM cell, at the imposed-flux boundary, not in the interior and not at the surface:
it is a boundary-layer flow, not a mode.  The emergent flux is right to 1e-4 everywhere
(the explicit K = 10 arm is 23-40 % wrong by t = 975 s) and the column carries the
imposed luminosity through all 84 cells.  And it costs HALF of the explicit K = 10 run
(2.0x faster per step, 10.7x hydro-only), because 13 explicit substeps with two PD-ARS
stages each are replaced by 13.5 Picard iterations of one tridiagonal solve.
The 2a diagnosis that the reduced speed of light was a suspect for the growing 72 s
oscillation is therefore SUPPORTED: removing it removes the growth.  (Note the residual
oscillation here has a period of ~50 s, not 72 s, and a constant amplitude.)
