M1 vs eddington: the diffusion closure is 8-100x more accurate on all three L1
  norms, which is the Skinner & Ostriker result (they report 1.7/6.1/7.8 % for M1
  with computed eigenvalues against 0.42/0.49/0.42 % for lambda = +-c/sqrt3); our
  M1 numbers are 4-10x better than theirs, and the ordering is the same.
RSLA: at chat/c = 0.1 nothing moves; at 0.01 the errors grow by 14 % (M0=2) and
  22 % (M0=5) RELATIVE, i.e. 0.40 -> 0.46 % and 0.30 -> 0.36 % in rho.  This is
  much smaller than PLUTO's >40 % at c/1000 because the STEADY state of the
  moment system is chat-independent (at d/dt = 0 the chat on the transport and
  the chat on the source cancel), so with the shock held in its own frame and
  started from the solution, RSLA can only show up through the transient and
  through terms of order v/chat -- and at c/1000 the supercritical v = 8.7e7 cm/s
  would EXCEED chat = 3e7, which is where PLUTO's number comes from.
N_sub: with subcycle = false (the default in the input) the mesh step IS the
  radiation step; with subcycle = true the hydro step is 2.28e-13 s against the
  radiation 3.99e-16 s, N_sub = 571, and the answer is the same to 1.6 % of the
  error itself.
