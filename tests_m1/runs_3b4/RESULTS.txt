WHAT bicgstab SOLVES.  Exactly the system line Jacobi iterates on: the assembled row
(TA,TB,TC,TR) of the Picard pass plus the four/six transverse off-diagonal coefficients
that TR's lagged term was built from, with b = TR + sum_nb C_nb E^k_nb.  One line-Jacobi
pass IS x <- M^{-1}(b - sum C x) with M the x1 line solve, so the two solvers have the
same matrix, the same right-hand side and the same fixed point.  bicgstab is right-
preconditioned by that same line solve (Thomas / cyclic Thomas / gathered stack), stops
on the TRUE residual max|b - A x| / max|b| < implicit_lin_tol, and reports outer passes,
inner iterations, breakdowns, fallbacks and global reductions at the end of the run.

G3  bicgstab == line_jacobi ON THE SAME PROBLEM
================================================================================
2-D 64^2, tau_cell = 1e3, implicit_cfl 1 and 1e4, the final double-precision x2 slice.
  g3 cfl=1      max relative difference of the final (E,F1,F2) slice = 5.252e-09
  g3 cfl=10000  max relative difference of the final (E,F1,F2) slice = 1.298e-10
The two solvers are NOT bitwise (they take different arithmetic to the same fixed point);
5e-9 and 1e-10 of the slice scale is agreement at the solver tolerance, and the looser
number is the CHEAP case, where BiCGStab stops after its very first preconditioner
application and line Jacobi takes three passes -- i.e. both are well inside the tolerance
and the difference is where each stops, not what each converges to.

       gather)              -1.240e-08   outer passes 5 / 5
BITWISE is NOT expected here as it was under line_jacobi: the dot products are Kokkos
reductions over each rank's own cells, so splitting the domain changes the summation
ORDER and therefore alpha, beta and omega in the last digits.  Measured, that costs
6e-16 -- two or three ULP -- and the energy change agrees to all eight printed digits
between the two decompositions.  dEtot/Etot = -1.24e-8 is the precision of the
single-precision `bin` writer the sum is taken from, as in 3b3 G4, not a drift.

  A first arm at vpert = 1.0e-2 was abandoned: same behaviour, 3x slower.

  With ANY horizontal structure the Picard loop stops converging.  Measured over the
  first six steps: outer passes pinned at implicit_maxit = 200 on every step, inner
  BiCGStab iterations 82.8 mean / 117 max, 24 breakdowns (all recovered by the restart,
  no line_jacobi fallback), and the final 7-point residual ~2e3 -- i.e. GROWING.  Over
  the 19-minute arm itself: 200/200 outer passes on every step and, at t = 1.91 s,
  max|v1| = 442 v_MLT, max|v2| = 247 v_MLT, max|v|/c_s = 6.8, min E on the floor (1e-4),
  F1top/F_in = 0.636.  The
  linear solver is doing its job; what has no fixed point is the OUTER iteration on the
  lagged off-diagonal Eddington terms sum_{e!=d} d_e P_de.  In the optically thin top of
  the slab those terms are multiplied by (c dt/dx)^2 ~ 7e3 (theta -> 1 there), so a
  purely explicit lagging of them cannot converge, and the run then walks away: at
  t = 1.3 s max|v1| is already 4e2 v_MLT.  Wall clock is 11-13 s/step,
  eight times the static value, because every step runs the full 200 passes.

  In ~19 minutes of wall clock the seeded arm reached t = 1.91 s = 0.004 turnover
  (H_p/v_MLT = 480 s), so NO growth rate, no F_rad/F_in(z) profile and no velocity
  structure can be claimed, and none is.  The time series that exist (KE_1, KE_2 and dt
  against time, and the last maps) are in impl3b4_heslab.json under `seeded`, as a record
  of the divergence and not as a physical result.

  THIS -- not the transverse diffusion -- IS WHAT BLOCKS A PRODUCTION 3-D RUN.
