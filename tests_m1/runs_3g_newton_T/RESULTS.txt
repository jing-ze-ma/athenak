WHAT WAS BUILT.  Two <rad_m1> options, BOTH DEFAULT false:

  implicit_gas_newton   (A)  T' is taken from the ELIMINATED (Schur) relation
                             B_k dT = R_k + c dt rho kappa_E E' that step (c)
                             already builds, instead of re-running the
                             bracketed root find in every Picard pass.  ZERO
                             EOS evaluations in step (f).  The converged state
                             solves the SAME nonlinear backward-Euler gas
                             equation (dT -> 0 forces R_k + c dt rho kappa_E E'
                             -> 0, with e(T) and T^4 exact at the final T), and
                             the gas energy is still set from q = SRCR - SRCB E'
                             so conservation is algebraically what it was.
                             Per-cell fallback to the bracketed root find on
                             c_v <= 0, T' <= 0, |dT| > 0.5 T_k, or a step that
                             does not decrease the exact nonlinear residual;
                             counted in ImplicitReport.
  implicit_eos_cache    (B)  rho is frozen over the step, so the density
                             direction of the tabulated e surface is collapsed
                             ONCE per cell into a 1-D cubic Hermite in ln T on
                             the TABLE'S OWN nodes (2*nt+1 cells,
                             implicit_eos_cache_nt, default 2).  A temperature
                             outside the window, an off-table density, a
                             sub-table temperature or eos_rad_taper fall back to
                             the real table and are counted as misses.
                             implicit_eos_cache_check (default true) makes one
                             TRUE-table evaluation per cell at the end of the
                             step and reports the accuracy.

WHAT DID NOT WORK / HONEST LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  A AND B ARE LARGELY REDUNDANT.  Once (A) removes the root find there is one
    table evaluation per cell-pass left, and building the cache costs about what
    it saves: A+B is within 0.3 % of A on the 2-D slab and 3 % on the 1-D
    column.  (B) is worth having on its own (1.14x / 1.54x) and as insurance for
    a configuration with many more passes; it is not additive with (A).
2.  NOT FASTER-CONVERGING.  The Picard count goes slightly UP with (A) (4.109 ->
    4.282 on the slab; unchanged at 11.709 on the 1-D column), because T is now
    one Newton step behind instead of an exact local root.  The outer loop is
    limited by the lagged closure and the transverse couplings, not by the gas
    nonlinearity.  ALL of the gain is per-pass cost.
3.  THE CACHE BARELY HELPS THE BRACKETED ROOT FIND: 5.19 misses per cell-pass
    with B alone, because the root find brackets by walking T <- T/2 (or 2T),
    which leaves a +-9.6 % window (nt = 2, table dlog = 0.02) on the first step.
    A window wide enough to hold the walk would cost more to build than it
    saves.  With (A) the walk is never taken and the miss rate is 0.
4.  FIRST ATTEMPT AT THE NEWTON SAFEGUARD FAILED: without a floor on the
    residual, "the step did not decrease |y|" fires in every converged cell (y
    is then a difference cancelling to round-off) and 13 % of all cell-passes
    fell back to the root find, costing a third of the gain.  Skipping the test
    below |y| = 1e-12 max(rho e^n, c dt rho kappa_E E') gives 0 fallbacks on
    every gate here.
5.  NOTHING IS CORRECTED at the end of a cached step.  The gas energy is set
    from the ASSEMBLED row, which is what makes e_gas + (c/chat) E change by the
    fluxes and the work term alone to round-off; re-evaluating q after the solve
    would break that balance.  Consistency with the real table is structural
    instead (the evolved state is (rho, e_gas), T' is not persistent, and the
    next step re-inverts e_gas through the real table), and the end-of-step
    true-table pass MEASURES the error rather than patching it.
6.  MEMORY.  iw grows by 5 components and, with the cache, ecache adds
    4(2nt+1)+1 = 21 Reals per cell at nt = 2.  Both only when the option is on.

HOW TO REPRODUCE

  bash run_gates.sh                                       (the G2 unit gates)
  python3 ../runs_3d_edd/an.py <dir>...                   (slab summary)
  inp/  holds copies of inputs/tests/rad_m1_*.athinput with the four new
  parameters written in at their defaults (a command-line override needs the
  parameter to exist in the file); he_slab_m1_2d_V3edd.athinput and
  he_box_m1_1d_impl.athinput here are the same copies of the runs_3d_edd /
  runs_3a inputs.  G1 was run against the UNTOUCHED originals, and repeated
  (g1a_new2, g1b_new2) against the FINAL binary: still byte-identical.
  All *.bin, tab/ and rst/ were deleted after the numbers above were measured
  (GPFS inode quota).
