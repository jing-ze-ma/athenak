CONCLUSION.  The mode is a kappa-mechanism at the Fe opacity bump that is ENABLED BY
THE REDUCED SPEED OF LIGHT.  It is not physical, and it is not the operator split.

 (a) physical strange/kappa mode : REFUTED as stated.  gamma is a non-monotonic
     function of chat with a maximum at K = 30 and falls to zero as chat -> c:
     5.7 / 8.5 / 4.2 / 0.6 / 0.05 e-3 at K = 10/30/100/300/1000.  A physical
     instability cannot care about chat.  The resonance condition explains the shape:
     the RSLA radiative diffusion time across the box is 781 s x (10/K) (PLAN.md), i.e.
     781 / 260 / 78 / 26 / 7.8 s at K = 10/30/100/300/1000, against a period P = 65 s;
     gamma peaks where t_diff ~ 4P and vanishes when t_diff << P.  At the TRUE speed of
     light t_diff = 2.2 s = 0.03 P: the box (cut at tau_bot = 100) is nowhere near the
     kappa-mechanism resonance, which needs the thermal time of the driving zone to
     equal the pulsation period.  So the driving is real physics (opacity + radiative
     heat exchange) applied to an artificially slowed radiation field.
 (b) RSLA : CONFIRMED as the enabling ingredient (the K scan above).
 (c) split lag : REFUTED.  gamma at cfl 0.3 / 0.15 / 0.075 is 8.54 / 8.59 / 8.61 e-3,
     flat to 1 % over a factor 4 in dt.  Not linear in dt, does not extrapolate to zero.
     (cfl_rad was not scanned separately: the substep is already ~13-400 per hydro step
     and dt_rad enters only through it; E4 `strang` is NOT implemented in this tree and
     there is no switch that moves the sub-cycle block relative to the hydro stage, so
     E4 was skipped.)
 (d) residual force / WB coupling : REFUTED as the driver.  Switching the whole
     Phi_eff + force_reference machinery OFF (k30_nowb) leaves gamma = 5.5e-3, the same
     order; what the machinery buys is the INITIAL KICK, 100x smaller in KEcol
     (1.1e29 -> 3.0e27 at t = 125 s), i.e. the G3 result of the 2a RESULTS restated.
 (e) boundaries : not the driver.  Production sponges (vdamp_top_tau = 0.1 /
     vdamp_top_time = 20 / vdamp_bot_cells = 12 / vdamp_bot_time = 20) remove only 4 %
     of gamma (8.19e-3 vs 8.54e-3): NET gamma STILL 8.2e-3, four times the convective
     rate.  The sponge cannot control this mode.
 (f) IC not an exact discrete equilibrium : this is the SEED, and only the seed.  With
     opac_freeze the column rings at constant amplitude (KEcol 1-5e27, |v1| ~ 3.4e4 cm/s
     = 1.8 v_MLT) for 1500 s and neither grows nor decays.

WHAT THE DRIVING NEEDS (all at K = 30):
  live opacity          required : opac_freeze -> gamma = -2.9e-4 (neutral) at K = 10,
                                   30 and 100 alike.
  momentum + work       required : dbg_gas_force = false -> gamma = -4.4e-4, KEcol flat
                                   at 2e25, two decades below every other arm.
  energy exchange       required : dbg_gas_heat = false -> no sustained growth
                                   (gamma = -1.4e-3 over 200-900 s).
  radiation passive     gamma ~ 0 : gas_feedback = false leaves |v1| < 0.03 v_MLT, so
                                   the hydro + WB potential part of the setup is clean.

WORK INTEGRAL (E8; k30_prof, rt_profile every 1.6 s, W_i = oint P_tot d(1/rho) with
P_tot = p_gas + E/3, per cycle, tables work_k30_t300-620.txt and _t150-470.txt).
  DRIVING (W > 0): cells 21-62, tau = 53 down to 1.6, peak at cells 37-39, tau 20-22,
    W*rho = 4.75e5 erg/cm^3/cycle.  That IS the Fe opacity bump (a_rad/g peaks at
    tau = 29.8).  Integrated over tau 5-60: +2.66e13 erg/cm^2/cycle.
  DAMPING (W < 0): the deep column tau > 55 (cells 0-20, -6.7e5 at the bottom wall) and
    the top cells tau < 1.3 (cells 63-83, -1e5 to -4.6e5).  The surface cells are
    nonlinear by then (drho/rho -> 1, |dv1| = 0.5 c_s) and dominate the raw sum, which
    is why the full-column sum is negative (-6.1e12) while tau > 2 gives +6.5e12.
  EIGENFUNCTION |dv1|(z): 0 at the bottom wall, 0.15 c_s at cell 25 (tau 45), a NODE at
    cell 47 (tau 9.9), then rising monotonically to 0.50 c_s at the top -> one node in
    the box, a surface-weighted fundamental radial mode.
  PHASE of dF1 relative to drho: -117 deg through the deep column, crossing zero at
    cell 22 (tau 51), +170 deg at the Fe bump, +50 deg at the surface.  The flux lags
    the compression by ~1/3 of a period through the driving zone: that lag IS the
    RSLA-slowed radiative response.

WHAT STAGE 2b SHOULD RUN WITH.
  The configuration is NOT ready: at K = 30 with production sponges gamma = 8.2e-3/s,
  gamma*P = 0.53, against the convective growth rate v_MLT/H_p = 2.1e-3/s.  The
  numerical mode grows FOUR TIMES faster than the convection that 2b is meant to study
  and reaches 0.5 c_s in ~700 s (0.2 turnover).  Options, in order:
   1. RAISE K until gamma << v_MLT/H_p.  K = 300 gives gamma = 6.3e-4 (0.3x the
      convective rate) and K = 1000 gives 5e-5 (0.02x).  Cost: N_sub ~ 40 K/30, so
      K = 300 is ~10x and K = 1000 ~33x the K = 30 cost.  In 3-D at tau_bot = 100 the
      PLAN's 12.9 node-h per 10 turnovers at K = 10 becomes ~390 node-h at K = 300.
      That is the honest price of a quiet column and it is probably unaffordable.
   2. CUT THE BOX SHALLOWER or move the bottom above the Fe bump, so that the driving
      zone is not inside the domain.  The work integral says all the driving is at
      tau = 5-60.
   3. Keep K = 30 and accept the mode, tracking it explicitly: it is a coherent 65-s
      radial mode with a known eigenfunction and can be projected out of the
      diagnostics.  NOT recommended before 2b is calibrated.
  Sponges: keep them (they cost nothing and damp the surface), but they are not a fix.
  Top BC: dark (now the same as vacuum, see below).  cfl: 0.3 is fine, the answer does
  not depend on it.
