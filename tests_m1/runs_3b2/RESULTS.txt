PHASE A1 -- the six <rad_m1> user-history columns under decomposition
---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/pgen/box_convection.cpp, BoxConvHistory, the m1_on_ branch.

THE DEFECT.  F1top / F1mid / F1bot and V1mid were read at each MeshBlock's OWN
i = ie / is / is + nx1/2.  With blocks tiled in x2/x3 that was already a proper plane
mean (`inc` divides by the MESH's nx2*nx3, so each column contributes once and the
history's MPI_SUM lands on the mean).  With blocks STACKED ALONG x1 every block
contributed its own end plane: the three flux columns came out part_nblk times too large
and mixed heights, and V1mid became a sum over part_nblk different depths.

THE FIX.  A per-block table gplane(m,0..2) built on the host from the block's logical
x1 location holds the LOCAL i of the GLOBAL bottom / mid / top plane, or -1 when this
block does not own it; the reduction tests against it.  Maxima (V1max, Fres) and volume
sums (Etot, KEcol) were always decomposition-correct and are untouched.

PHASE A2 -- is box_convection block-decomposition invariant along x1?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT: there is NO BUG in the code paths the M1 box uses.  The non-invariance is
ROUND-OFF IN THE MESH CELL-CENTRE COORDINATES, amplified ~1e7 by the fact that the total
x1 momentum of a hydrostatic column starts at exactly zero and stays ~1e-8 of the force
terms that cancel into it.  Nothing in wb_phi_eff, the a_rad_ref fill, the M1 IC reader,
the bc_mode = 3 wall walk or the BC branch treats a MeshBlock's x1 extent as the column.

THE MEASUREMENT (he_box_m1_1d_impl.athinput, meshblock/nx1 = 84 vs 42, nlim = 1,
hydro_u and m1 tab dumps compared cell by cell after sorting on x1v):

  dump 0 (the INITIAL CONDITION, before any step; max relative difference)
      x1v   8.19e-14      <- the cell CENTRES already differ, ~2 ulp
      dens  6.48e-15   ener 6.33e-15   mom1 0.0
      M1 E  1.98e-15   M1 F1 0.0
  dump 1 (after ONE cycle)
      dens  5.54e-15   ener 4.53e-11   mom1 1.76e-06   (84 of 84 cells, not localised
                                                        at the block interface i = 42)
      M1 E  4.20e-12   M1 F1 8.66e-12   (identical in size with transport = explicit:
                                         3.05e-12 / 2.26e-13, so it is NOT the gather
                                         partition)

WHY THE COORDINATES DIFFER.  x1v = CellCenterX(i-is, nx1, x1min, x1max) is evaluated from
each MeshBlock's OWN (x1min, x1max, nx1); the block edges are not exact binary fractions
of the mesh, so the same physical cell centre comes out ~2 ulp apart under a different
decomposition.  The pgen interpolates the initial column, the M1 IC and a_rad_ref at
those coordinates, so the initial state differs at 5e-15.  This is a generic AthenaK
property, not a box_convection one.

THE CONTROL that settles it.  A SINGLE-BLOCK run with mesh/x1min moved by ONE ULP
(-1.8149339e8 -> -181493390.00000006) reproduces the same signature:

                                       x1v       dens      mom1 (1 cycle)   ener
  1 block, x1min + 1 ulp            5.43e-15   4.36e-15     5.00e-08      1.14e-12
  1 vs 2 blocks                     8.19e-14   6.48e-15     1.76e-06      4.53e-11
i.e. a coordinate perturbation 15x smaller gives a momentum perturbation 35x smaller, in
the same channel and with the same "every cell, not the interface" pattern.  The
amplification from the IC perturbation to mom1 after one step is ~1e7 in both.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE GATE.  "1 vs 2 vs 4 blocks bitwise" is NOT achievable for this
problem and never was; the honest statement is: mass and total energy agree to round-off
(1.4e-15 / 4e-16 relative over 60 s), the total x1 momentum -- a near-exact cancellation
that starts at zero -- agrees to 1.9e-5 RELATIVE, i.e. 3e14 absolute on a 1e21 scale,
seeded by a 5e-15 coordinate round-off.  The implicit partition contributes nothing to
this: the Picard statistics are identical to the last digit for 1, 2 and 4 blocks, and
the explicit transport shows the SAME divergence (3.2e-4 in 3b's measurement).

NOT FIXED, REPORTED (out of scope, mesh-wide):  making x1v decomposition-invariant would
mean computing cell centres from the GLOBAL mesh extents and the global index rather than
from the block's own edges.  That would make this and every other stratified pgen
reproducible under decomposition, and is the only way the gate above becomes bitwise.

ALSO REPORTED, NOT TOUCHED (a two-stream-only path, as the brief directed): the non-M1
branch of BoxConvHistory reads the emergent flux at fb(m,b,ie+1,k,j), the block's own top
face, and its comment states the assumption "one MeshBlock spans the whole x1 extent".
It has the same defect as A1 had, and would need the same treatment before a two-stream
run is decomposed along x1.
